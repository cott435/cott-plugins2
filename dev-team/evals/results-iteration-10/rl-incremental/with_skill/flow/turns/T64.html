### AD-05: Seeds, test-seed reservation and CPU determinism
**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Scope:** system
- **Context:** RUN-03 needs identical curves on a rerun. SNAKE-08, TANK-13, HUNT-15, TAB-03, DQN-06, PG-07 and REPLAY-07 need repeatable games. EVAL-01 needs test seeds never used in training. - **Decision:**
  - **Streams:** a run seed builds a `np.random.SeedSequence`. Its first child gives training game seeds. Each side then gets three children of its own: move choice (exploration or sampling from the policy), batch sampling, and network initialisation (a `torch.Generator`). One last child gives opponent draws (AD-17). Children are spawned in that order, side by side in `Env.sides` order, so a one-side run's streams don't depend on what other environments use.
  - **Seed ranges:** test seeds are 0–99; training game seeds are drawn from `[1_000, 2^31)`.
  - **Torch:** runs with `torch.set_num_threads(1)` and `torch.use_deterministic_algorithms(True)`.

## Data-source research: Sharadar and a second daily price source (read 2026-10-17)

**Main finding:** Sharadar now sells and documents its data directly at sharadar.com, with its own API (`api.sharadar.com/v1.0`). Its own guide says "It is not Nasdaq Data Link" and says not to use a sharadar.com key with `nasdaqdatalink` (https://sharadar.com/llms.txt). I couldn't read Nasdaq Data Link's own Sharadar pages: data.nasdaq.com/databases/SEP and /SFA load only an empty page without JavaScript, and docs.data.nasdaq.com redirects to /accesstools. So the Sharadar facts below come from sharadar.com. Whether they also hold for your Data Link subscription is unconfirmed (probe P1).

### Part A — Sharadar

**1. SEP (stock prices, now called `stocks`)** (https://sharadar.com/docs/stocks)
- **Fields:** ticker, date, open, high, low, close, volume, closeadj, closeunadj, lastupdated.
- **Adjustment:**
  - open/high/low/close/volume are adjusted for splits only.
  - `closeadj` is adjusted for splits, cash dividends and spinoffs.
  - `closeunadj` is not adjusted.
- **History and coverage:** history starts December 1997. The table covers "active and delisted US public stocks", about 21,000 tickers. Data is delivered daily at 17:30 and 23:30 ET.
- **Rebuilding other prices:** unadjusted open = open × closeunadj / close. Volume is not adjusted for dividends or spinoffs (https://sharadar.com/docs/faqs).

**2. ACTIONS (`actions`)** (https://sharadar.com/docs/actions)
- **Fields:** date, action, ticker, name, value, contraticker, contraname. History starts January 1998.
- **Action types:** the data dictionary lists 75 (pulled with the public sample key from `https://api.sharadar.com/v1.0/data/descriptions?api_key=test-api-key&table=actiontypes`). They include:
  - split, dividend, spinoff, spinoffdividend
  - tickerchangefrom/tickerchangeto, listed, delisted, voluntarydelisting, regulatorydelisting, bankruptcyliquidation
  - acquisitionby, acquisitioncash, acquisitionstock, acquisitionelectcash/stock, mergerfrom/mergerto, relation, exchangefrom/to, sicchangefrom/to
  - many acquisition variants such as "besteffort", "estimated" and per-currency cash.
- **Meaning of key types:**
  - For `dividend`, value is the cash amount adjusted for splits, and date is the ex-date.
  - For `split`, value is shares held after per share held before.
  - For the delisting types, date is the last trade date.

**3. TICKERS (`tickers`)** (https://sharadar.com/docs/tickers)
- **Fields:** permaticker ("a unique and unchanging identifier for a security (share class)"), isdelisted (Y/N), category, sector, industry, sicsector, famaindustry, firstpricedate, lastpricedate, firstadded, cusips, figi, relatedtickers.
- **Ticker reuse:** when a ticker is reused, "the active company keeps the ticker and the delisted company gets a number appended". When a ticker changes, the history moves to the new ticker (https://sharadar.com/docs/faqs).

**4. SP500 (`sp500`)** (https://sharadar.com/docs/sp500)
- **Contents:** current members, additions and removals with effective dates, plus quarterly snapshots of the index.
- **Fields:** date, action, ticker, name, contraticker, contraname, note. History starts January 1998.
- **Plans:** "Included in Fundamentals, Prices, Bundle", so it is not a separate add-on on sharadar.com.

**5. Access**
- **sharadar.com:** bulk download with `years=5|10|full`, which redirects to a zip file; the docs advise against paging through a whole table (https://sharadar.com/docs/stocks, https://sharadar.com/llms.txt). The default row limit is 10,000 (https://sharadar.com/docs/getting-started). I found no published rate limit.
- **Data Link Python client:** installed with `pip install nasdaq-data-link`. `get_table(..., paginate=True)` reads several pages, and `export_table()` downloads a filtered zip ("`cursor_id` and `paginate` are not supported for exporting"). It retries automatically on HTTP 429 (https://github.com/Nasdaq/data-link-python/blob/main/FOR_ANALYSTS.md, https://github.com/Nasdaq/data-link-python/blob/main/README.md).
- **Data Link rate limits:**
  - Observed directly: an anonymous request returned "anonymous user limit of 50 calls per day" (data.nasdaq.com API).
  - From a search snippet of https://docs.data.nasdaq.com/docs/rate-limits-1, which now redirects so I couldn't read it: about 2,000 calls per 10 minutes for free keys, 5,000 per 10 minutes and 720,000 per day for table subscribers, and 60 per hour for `qopts.export`. Treat these as unverified.

**6. Delisting return**
- Sharadar provides no delisting return. You get the last price, the last trade date, and a delisting action whose value is the final market cap.
- The action type works as a reason code: voluntary, regulatory, bankruptcy/liquidation, acquisition or merger.
- Cash and stock paid per share in an acquisition are in `acquisitioncash` and `acquisitionstock`. The FAQ estimates about 99% coverage and excludes contingent value rights (https://sharadar.com/docs/faqs).

**Recommendation: EODHD Historian at $19.99/month.** It is the only candidate within budget that documents all of the following: delisted US tickers, raw close, adjusted close, a splits and dividends API, and 30+ years of history. Tiingo Power ($30) is the fallback; it has the cleanest fields and CRSP-style adjustment, but its docs say nothing about delisted coverage.

For the 1bp check, don't compare one vendor's adjusted close directly to the other's. EODHD says adjusted closes "are recomputed, not stored", and the two vendors may adjust differently. Instead, build daily returns from each vendor's raw close, dividends and splits, and compare those. The 0.5% check compares raw close to Sharadar's `closeunadj`.

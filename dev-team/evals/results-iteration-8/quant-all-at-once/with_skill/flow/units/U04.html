Institutional Investors Summary by Investor Frequently Asked Questions How are ticker changes handled? The ticker is a unique identifier for a security. When a ticker changes we rewire the history for the updated ticker. When a company is delisted and its ticker is later reused by a different company, the active company keeps the ticker and the delisted company gets a number appended. Historical ticker changes are recorded in the actions table. In addition, we provide a permaticker in the tickers table that is Sharadar’s own unchanging and unique identifier for a security. Separate share classes of the same issuer receive separate permatickers. What does the tickers category field represent? Category combines SEC filing origin (Domestic form 10; ADR form 20; Canadian form 40) with instrument type (common stock, preferred, ETF, CEF, ETN, ETD, and related forms) and share-class role where relevant (Primary Class or Secondary Class). The current vocabulary is on the tickers table metadata. Institutional Investor is used for holdings_investor entity rows, not listed securities. There is no warrant category. Do you provide acquisition share/cash consideration? Yes. Cash and stock consideration for acquisitions and mergers is in the actions table. acquisitioncash is cash received per share (USD). acquisitionstock is the number of shares of the acquiring or surviving security received for each share of the acquired or non-surviving company. When both cash and stock were received, the two actions are additive. Contingent value rights are not included. When holders could elect cash or stock, acquisitionelectcash and acquisitionelectstock record the alternatives (one, not both). We estimate about 99% coverage of acquisition and merger consideration and are continuously improving that. Field-level detail is in the descriptions table where the table field equals actiontypes. The current SIC is on the tickers table. Field-level detail is in the descriptions table where the table field equals actiontypes. How are company restatements handled within the fundamentals dataset? The fundamentals table keeps as-reported and restated financials as separate reporting dimensions. As-Reported (ARQ, ARY, ART) is a point-in-time view, time-indexed to the SEC form 10 filing date, and excludes restatements. That view is not rewritten when a later filing restates a prior period; the later filing creates a new observation. As-Reported is typically suitable for back-testing. Most-Recent Reported (MRQ, MRY, MRT) is time-indexed to the report period and is updated when a company restates prior periods. # Sharadar

> Nearly 30 years of reference-grade US public company data - fundamentals, EOD stock and fund prices, corporate actions, securities master, institutional holdings, and insider transactions. Point-in-time and survivorship-bias-free. Delivered via REST API or bulk CSV zip download.

Sharadar (https://sharadar.com) is a direct data service for quants, analysts, and builders. Tables are known by descriptive names (`fundamentals`, `stocks`, ...) and by well-known legacy codes (`SF1`, `SEP`, ...) that people search for. Both work in the API and docs. Full product documentation lives under `/docs`.

## Getting started

This is the direct Sharadar API: `https://api.sharadar.com/v1.0`. It is not Nasdaq Data Link. Do not call `data.nasdaq.com` or use the `nasdaqdatalink` / `quandl` libraries with a sharadar.com key.

You need an API key from https://sharadar.com/account after sign-in. If there is no key, or a request returns 401, send the user to https://sharadar.com/subscribe (guest checkout is allowed). Do not invent access.

Pass the key as `api_key` or `apiKey` on the query string, or as header `x-api-key`. Do not ask the user to paste the key into chat or hardcode it.

The documented `test-api-key` only returns the free sample universe (roughly DJIA names such as AAPL). A 403 `Exceeds free tier` means that request is running as free-tier, not as a paid key.

For an entire table, use bulk: add `years=5`, `years=10`, or `years=full` to `GET /data/{table}`. The API responds 302 to a zip. `tickers` and `descriptions` only publish a full snapshot; `years=5` and `years=10` still download that file. Do not page a full table with `limit` and `skip` / `offset`. Use `ticker`, `from`, `to`, and `fields` for slices. Delisted tickers are in the same tables (for example `ticker=TWO` on `/data/stocks`). Dates are calendar dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`); fundamentals also has `calendardate` vs `date`.

## Data tables

Each table has a modern name and, where applicable, a legacy code (same data either way). Legacy codes such as **SF1** and **SEP** are widely searched and remain first-class.

## Data-source research: Sharadar and a second daily price source (read 2026-10-17)

**Main finding:** Sharadar now sells and documents its data directly at sharadar.com, with its own API (`api.sharadar.com/v1.0`). Its own guide says "It is not Nasdaq Data Link" and says not to use a sharadar.com key with `nasdaqdatalink` (https://sharadar.com/llms.txt). I couldn't read Nasdaq Data Link's own Sharadar pages: data.nasdaq.com/databases/SEP and /SFA load only an empty page without JavaScript, and docs.data.nasdaq.com redirects to /accesstools. So the Sharadar facts below come from sharadar.com. Whether they also hold for your Data Link subscription is unconfirmed (probe P1).

### Part A — Sharadar

**1. SEP (stock prices, now called `stocks`)** (https://sharadar.com/docs/stocks)
- **Fields:** ticker, date, open, high, low, close, volume, closeadj, closeunadj, lastupdated.
- **Adjustment:**
  - open/high/low/close/volume are adjusted for splits only.
  - `closeadj` is adjusted for splits, cash dividends and spinoffs.
  - `closeunadj` is not adjusted.
- **History and coverage:** history starts December 1997. The table covers "active and delisted US public stocks", about 21,000 tickers. Data is delivered daily at 17:30 and 23:30 ET.
- **Rebuilding other prices:** unadjusted open = open × closeunadj / close. Volume is not adjusted for dividends or spinoffs (https://sharadar.com/docs/faqs).

**2. ACTIONS (`actions`)** (https://sharadar.com/docs/actions)
- **Fields:** date, action, ticker, name, value, contraticker, contraname. History starts January 1998.
- **Action types:** the data dictionary lists 75 (pulled with the public sample key from `https://api.sharadar.com/v1.0/data/descriptions?api_key=test-api-key&table=actiontypes`). They include:
  - split, dividend, spinoff, spinoffdividend
  - tickerchangefrom/tickerchangeto, listed, delisted, voluntarydelisting, regulatorydelisting, bankruptcyliquidation
  - acquisitionby, acquisitioncash, acquisitionstock, acquisitionelectcash/stock, mergerfrom/mergerto, relation, exchangefrom/to, sicchangefrom/to
  - many acquisition variants such as "besteffort", "estimated" and per-currency cash.
- **Meaning of key types:**
  - For `dividend`, value is the cash amount adjusted for splits, and date is the ex-date.
  - For `split`, value is shares held after per share held before.
  - For the delisting types, date is the last trade date.

**3. TICKERS (`tickers`)** (https://sharadar.com/docs/tickers)
- **Fields:** permaticker ("a unique and unchanging identifier for a security (share class)"), isdelisted (Y/N), category, sector, industry, sicsector, famaindustry, firstpricedate, lastpricedate, firstadded, cusips, figi, relatedtickers.
- **Ticker reuse:** when a ticker is reused, "the active company keeps the ticker and the delisted company gets a number appended". When a ticker changes, the history moves to the new ticker (https://sharadar.com/docs/faqs).

**4. SP500 (`sp500`)** (https://sharadar.com/docs/sp500)
- **Contents:** current members, additions and removals with effective dates, plus quarterly snapshots of the index.
- **Fields:** date, action, ticker, name, contraticker, contraname, note. History starts January 1998.
- **Plans:** "Included in Fundamentals, Prices, Bundle", so it is not a separate add-on on sharadar.com.

**5. Access**
- **sharadar.com:** bulk download with `years=5|10|full`, which redirects to a zip file; the docs advise against paging through a whole table (https://sharadar.com/docs/stocks, https://sharadar.com/llms.txt). The default row limit is 10,000 (https://sharadar.com/docs/getting-started). I found no published rate limit.
- **Data Link Python client:** installed with `pip install nasdaq-data-link`. `get_table(..., paginate=True)` reads several pages, and `export_table()` downloads a filtered zip ("`cursor_id` and `paginate` are not supported for exporting"). It retries automatically on HTTP 429 (https://github.com/Nasdaq/data-link-python/blob/main/FOR_ANALYSTS.md, https://github.com/Nasdaq/data-link-python/blob/main/README.md).
- **Data Link rate limits:**
  - Observed directly: an anonymous request returned "anonymous user limit of 50 calls per day" (data.nasdaq.com API).
  - From a search snippet of https://docs.data.nasdaq.com/docs/rate-limits-1, which now redirects so I couldn't read it: about 2,000 calls per 10 minutes for free keys, 5,000 per 10 minutes and 720,000 per day for table subscribers, and 60 per hour for `qopts.export`. Treat these as unverified.

**6. Delisting return**
- Sharadar provides no delisting return. You get the last price, the last trade date, and a delisting action whose value is the final market cap.
- The action type works as a reason code: voluntary, regulatory, bankruptcy/liquidation, acquisition or merger.
- Cash and stock paid per share in an acquisition are in `acquisitioncash` and `acquisitionstock`. The FAQ estimates about 99% coverage and excludes contingent value rights (https://sharadar.com/docs/faqs).

- **P4:** What are the actual rate limits on your Data Link key?
- **P5:** Does SP500 store tickers as they were at the time, or the current renamed ones?

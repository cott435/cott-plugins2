### AD-03: A drawdown carries its trough date
**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Scope:** risk
- **Context:** RISK-05 reports the deepest fall with the date of its peak and of its trough, and REP-01 writes both dates; TC-08 checks both. The `risk.Drawdown` frozen in M1-T01 held only `peak_date`, so M1-T20 could not meet TC-08 and the report could not write the trough.
- **Decision:** `risk.Drawdown` gains `trough_date: str`; `max_drawdown` sets it to the date the deepest fall bottoms out (the first if tied), and `write_report` writes it in each row beside `peak_date`.
- **Alternatives:** a separate `trough_dates` mapping returned beside the drawdowns — two values for one fact, kept in step by hand.
- **Consequences:** a contract change mid-M1: the `risk.Drawdown` and `risk.Report` samples and contract test are re-frozen by a new interface task before M1-T20; `write_report`'s rows gain `trough_date`.
- **Probe:** none — no external API, vendor or dataset involved.

<!-- docs/spec.md:33 -->
- **RISK-05** Maximum drawdown comes with its dates.
  - Given closes, when the drawdown is computed, then wealth starts at 1 on the first date, and the result is the deepest fall below the running peak (trough / peak − 1, at most 0) with the date of that peak and the date of the trough; the first if tied. With no fall, both dates are the first date.

<!-- docs/spec.md:40 -->
- **REP-01** The risk report is a JSON file.
  - Given a prices CSV, when the report runs, then `{"tickers": [...]}` is written with one row per ticker, sorted, holding the RISK-04 fields and RISK-05's drawdown (as `max_drawdown`) and both dates.

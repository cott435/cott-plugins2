# FinTrack — Brief

**Status:** Accepted · **Last updated:** 2026-10-15

## Problem
I track spending by hand from bank exports. I want my bank and card transactions pulled in daily and categorized.

## Goals
1. My accounts sync daily without manual exports.
2. Every transaction lands in one ledger with a category.
3. Foreign-currency purchases show in dollars.

## Not doing
- Budgets, bill pay, or anything that moves money.
- Other users.

## Open questions
- Do my banks work with the bank-linking service's free tier, and how much history does each return? Answered by probing the service during architecture.

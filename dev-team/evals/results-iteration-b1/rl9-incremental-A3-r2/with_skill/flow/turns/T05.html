209:## Changelog
- **Full-grid timing (QR-03):** the CNN rung may need more than 60 CPU minutes to beat random. Mitigation: run the timing test first in that rung's milestone. Fall back to the flattened-grid MLP (AD-05), then to rented hardware within QR-05.
- **Scripted player too strong (EVAL-04):** every rung may tie the scripted player. Mitigation: reward and step-budget settings are per run (SNAKE-06), and the grid rung has the most room to beat it.
- **Matching the reference (REF-01):** Stable-Baselines3 has defaults the hand-written agents lack (DQN's gradient clipping at 10 and Huber loss; PPO's advantage normalisation and value-loss weight). Mitigation: the mapping in `reference` lists every one, and the hand-written agents adopt the same values, so a gap is the learner's code rather than a hidden setting.
- **Escape too easy or too hard (EVAL-07):** if fish at 0.8 × the ball's speed survive the cap easily, or never, the rungs can't separate from the scripted fish. Mitigation: the spec's numbers are tank constants; a measurement run of the two baselines comes first in S2's build, and a change to the numbers goes back through spec-writing.
- **The teams revision ripples into M1 (AD-13):** M1's pipelines and interface task were planned against one team per environment. Mitigation: they're revised and re-approved before `/build`; the expected values of TC-01–TC-49 don't change.
- **Self-play inside 60 minutes (QR-07):** two PPO learners sharing one run's budget may not reach a visible rung-2 failure, or a rung-3 fix. Mitigation: a timing run comes first in S3's build. The world numbers are spec constants, so any change goes back through spec-writing.
- **Cross-play cost:** 5,000 games of up to 1,000 steps with 7 animals is several minutes per run, and isn't covered by a QR. Mitigation: it's a separate command; measure it in S3's build and propose a QR if it hurts.
- **Package size:** 12 components and a contract of about 450 lines are at the Grow guides. AD-01 keeps one package. The natural seam, if one is needed, is `selfplay` with predator-prey.

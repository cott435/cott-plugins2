I've marked the `qfeat` pipelines **Approved** and made the five contract changes as an architecture revision. The `qfeat` contract and the root architecture are back to **Draft** for your review. Lint now shows 0 errors, so the 24 pipeline errors are gone.

The two remaining warnings are contract length: `qdata` at 209 lines and `qfeat` at 208, both over the 200-line guideline. AD-12 now records keeping both whole, because `dt context` loads only the sections a task reads.

## The five contract changes (`docs/packages/qfeat/contract.md`)
1. **Best feature.**
   - New `claims.BestFeature` type, with name, mean rank correlation, and the number of dates it was chosen on.
   - New `claims.best_feature`: the candidate with the highest mean, never a calibration feature. Ties go to the first in list order.
   - New `claims.model_minus_best`: adds the model-minus-best series.
   - `information_coefficients` no longer takes `best`. `FeatureList.best` and `Registry.register` now use `BestFeature`.
2. **`panel.holdout`:** returns holdout rows only, for scoring. Training still sees the full panel in date order.
3. **`explore.CharacterisationInputs`:** the data version, plus four development-only tables:
   - quantiles and tail shares per feature;
   - missing share per feature and year;
   - feature-to-feature rank correlations;
   - each feature's mean rank correlation per 5-year block.
4. **Named panel features:** `FeaturePanel` lists its feature columns in its metadata, calibration ones last. That's the 9 named features for real data, or `planted` plus `noise_1` to `noise_8` for the synthetic panel. `model` and `claims` read the list from there. A synthetic test case was added for it.
5. **Rename:** `ICSeries` is now `IcSeries`, and its schema `ICSchema` is now `IcSchema`.

## Also in the root architecture
- **AD-12, revised in place:** the title is now "Packages stay single past three pipelines while they share one install". Its scope adds `qfeat` (7 pipelines, sharing one panel, model and claim family), and its rejected alternatives add splitting the registry from the runs.
- **Changelog:** one new row.

There's no new decision: these are interface changes with no technical choice behind them. Their consumers are the `qfeat` pipelines you just approved, which already use the new shapes, so nothing goes back to pipeline-design.

## Assumptions I made
- **Field names:** the four `CharacterisationInputs` tables are called `distributions`, `missing`, `correlations` and `ic_by_block`.
- **Tie rule:** first in list order wins.

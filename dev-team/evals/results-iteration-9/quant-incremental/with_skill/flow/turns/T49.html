The commit was already made by the end of the last turn (`76fd360`), so nothing was waiting. Now the S4 spec. Nothing is written into the spec until you've answered the questions below.

## 1. Conflicts with the approved spec
- **Out-of-scope lines to narrow:**
  - "Trading on forecasts: return-seeking backtests, portfolios built from forecasts, and paper trading" becomes "Learned allocation agents, any broker, and real-money trading".
  - "Deep learning and automated feature search" becomes "Automated feature search; neural models other than S4's forecasters".
  - "Transaction costs and turnover limits; turnover is reported only" becomes "Turnover limits. S2's study reports turnover without costs; S4's backtests and paper book charge 5 basis points of turnover".
- **PORT-07 (S2 portfolios formed at the rebalance close):** unchanged for the S2 study. In the paper book, S2's portfolios follow the book's fill rule, a new requirement, so every portfolio in the book is treated the same.
- **LOAD-02 (refresh):** unchanged. Refreshes stay user-started, since the brief rules out scheduled jobs. A skipped day is caught up on the next refresh, with every decision still read as of its own date.
- **S3 HOLD:** unchanged. S4 uses S3's registered list and its verdicts, and never runs S3's holdout itself.
- **Summary, Users and Scope** get rewritten for four stages, and a new QR-11 covers training time.

## 2. Goal → feature mapping (all Goal 4, Stage S4)
| Feature | Code | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Forecast baselines | `BASE` | 12-month mean and 12-1 momentum for return; exponentially weighted volatility (λ = 0.94) |
| Neural forecaster | `FCST` | Sequence model on daily history plus S3's surviving features; walk-forward, trained locally |
| Forecast evaluation | `FEVAL` | Each neural forecast against its baseline on development years, tested with `null-hypothesis-testing`; error characterisation with `data-analysis` |
| Forecast portfolios | `FPORT` | Top fifth by forecast return weighted by inverse forecast volatility; risk parity with forecast volatilities; 5 basis-point costs; development backtest beside S2's portfolios |
| Frozen setup | `FRZ` | Models, rules and the retraining schedule registered before paper trading |
| Paper book | `PAPER` | Daily marks on each refresh, month-end decisions as of that date, next-day fills net of costs, append-only record |
| Verdict | `VERD` | After 12 months: Sharpe after costs for each forecast portfolio against each comparison, one corrected family, with the detectable effect |

## 3. Assumptions (correct me or I'll go ahead)
1. **Dates and universe:** S2's monthly rebalance dates and eligibility rules (STRUCT-01, STRUCT-02); exits handled as PORT-08.
2. **Model inputs:** the last 63 trading days of daily return, log dollar volume and daily range, plus the S3 features whose claims passed on the holdout. If none passed, history alone, and the report says so.
3. **Targets:** next-month market-removed return, ranked as in S3. Volatility is the holding month's realized volatility of total returns, annualized.
4. **Training:** walk-forward, retrained each January on dates at least two months earlier. Predictions start in 2005, and development covers every backtest year to the data's end.
5. **Development scoring:** return forecasts by mean monthly rank correlation; volatility forecasts by QLIKE loss (a standard loss for scoring variance forecasts); each against its baseline.
6. **Model size and time:** at most about 1 million parameters. A full walk-forward training run finishes within 2 hours on your laptop (QR-11).
7. **Costs:** 5 basis points × one-way turnover (as PORT-09), charged at each fill to every portfolio.
8. **Frozen setup:** registered with a content-hash ID, as S3's lists are. Paper trading starts at the first rebalance date after registration, and retrains on the registered schedule only.
9. **The paper book:** append-only, one record per portfolio per trading day, never edited.
10. **The verdict:** after 12 holding months, using daily returns after costs. Each claim's test and null come from `null-hypothesis-testing` at 5% after correction, and the report states the smallest difference in Sharpe the 12 months could detect.

## 4. Questions
1. **Fills:** next trading day's open (my default), or next day's close?
2. **Viewing the paper book:** reports only for now (my default), or an explorer page?
3. **Which S2 portfolios join the book?** My proposal is minimum variance, risk parity and HRP on the shrinkage estimate, plus equal weight: 4 comparisons × 2 forecast portfolios = 8 claims. All nine full-set S2 portfolios would mean 20 claims and less power each.
4. **Neural versus baseline, forward too?** My proposal: the book also runs the same two portfolios driven by the *baseline* forecasts. Then the forward test also answers whether the neural model beats simple forecasting after costs. That adds 2 portfolios and makes 16 claims. The alternative is neural portfolios only.
5. **If a neural forecast doesn't beat its baseline on development,** does it still go into the paper book? My proposal is yes, with the development verdict recorded at registration, so the forward test isn't chosen by the backtest.

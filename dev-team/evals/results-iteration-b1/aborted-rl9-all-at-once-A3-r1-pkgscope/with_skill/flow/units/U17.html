145	**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Scope:** system
146	- **Context:** TRAIN-06 needs every learning method to learn a hand-derived best behaviour before it is used in a world. Bugs in RL are silent (brief), and methods change while a world is built.
147	- **Decision:** `train.run_check` trains each team method in a config on its check world: for DQN a 6-state corridor with two exits, left of state 0 (reward 0.8) and right of state 5 (reward 1.0), γ = 0.9, so the best action is left in states 0–1 and right in 2–5 and a learner biased to one action can't pass; for PPO a 1-D point that is rewarded for reaching a target, where the best push is the sign of the offset.

Goals

goalG1Trust every result. Nothing uses information from after its date, companies that left the index stay in, and no claim counts until it beats chance.
served byEVAL, FEAT, FTEST, ING, LOAD, PAPER, UNIV, VAL
goalG2Judge every method against simpler baselines on the same unseen data and trading costs. A complex method must beat every simpler one.
served byAGENT, EVAL, FCST, FPORT, FTEST, PAPER, PORT
goalG3Get risk right before predicting returns. Know which correlations between stocks are real and which are noise.
served byCOV, EDA, FACT, PORT
goalG4See the data and results in a browser, including long download and checking runs as they happen.
served byAPP, EDA

requirementCOV-01The system produces a sample covariance estimate per window.added
criteria
  • Given a window with T days and N stocks, when the estimate is made, then it is symmetric and matches a direct calculation within 1e-10.
requirementCOV-02The system produces a shrinkage estimate that pulls the sample covariance toward a structured target, with the shrinkage amount estimated from the data.added
criteria
  • Given a window, when the shrinkage estimate is made, then the shrinkage amount (between 0 and 1) is recorded, and the estimate is positive definite.
requirementCOV-03The system produces a denoised estimate. Correlation eigenvalues inside the Marchenko–Pastur noise band are replaced by their average, keeping the trace. The result is then turned back into a covariance.added
criteria
  • Given a window, when the denoised estimate is made, then eigenvalues above the edge are unchanged, the trace of the correlation matrix equals N within 1e-8, and the estimate is positive definite.
  • Given simulated pure-noise returns, when denoised, then the denoised correlation matrix is closer to the identity (smaller Frobenius distance) than the sample one.
requirementCOV-04The estimation window length defaults to 756 trading days, and 504 and 1260 can also be chosen.added
criteria
  • Given a window length setting, when estimates are made, then each window uses exactly that many trading days.
requirementCOV-05The estimators are compared on real data with the data-analysis skill. It is given each estimator's out-of-sample minimum-variance portfolio variance per rebalance, and its out-of-sample covariance forecast error per window, over the development period.added
criteria
  • Given a completed S2 walk-forward run, when the comparison runs, then the report shows each estimator's realized variance and forecast error, with the spread over time and its stability across sub-periods (2005–2009, 2010–2014, 2015–2019, 2020 to the holdout start).
requirementCOV-06Any claim that one estimator beats another is tested with the null-hypothesis-testing skill. It is given the paired per-rebalance realized-variance differences over the development period. The claim passes only if the null of no difference is rejected at 5%, after correction for all estimator pairs compared.added
criteria
  • Given the claim "denoised beats sample", when tested, then the report states the test, the p-value, the corrected verdict and the effect size.

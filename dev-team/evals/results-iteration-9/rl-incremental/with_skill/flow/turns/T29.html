### Conflicts with the current spec (proposed fixes)
- **Out of scope**, "competitive or cooperative multi-agent learning…" → **"Cooperative multi-agent learning. Fish-tank fish are independent copies sharing one policy."**
- **CORE-01** (one batch of agents, one observation shape) → an environment declares **teams**. Each team has its own agents, observation shape and action space, and a step takes one action array per team. Snake and the fish tank are one-team environments. *(changed)*
- **RUN-02** (a final checkpoint) → also, when the settings ask for it, **each team's checkpoint every 1/10 of the run, saved as generations**. *(changed)*
- **REPLAY-03** (record new games from a checkpoint) → from **any pairing of team checkpoints**, including generation *i* against generation *j*. *(changed)*
- **EVAL-02** (every agent beats the random player) → in predator-prey, each learned side beats a **random opponent-side player**, measured against the same scripted opposition. *(changed)*
- **REF-01–03**: the compared rung for predator-prey is the **predator** trained against scripted prey. The prey side is 6 agents, which the library can't run, so the predator is the only fair single-agent case. *(changed)*
- **QR-07** (≤ 60 min per fish-tank rung) → widened to every fish-tank and predator-prey run. *(changed)*

### Goal → feature map (all S3)
```
Goal 1 → Predator-prey world (HUNT), Self-play training (SELF)
Goal 2 → REF widened (predator vs scripted prey, compared with library PPO)
Goal 3 → Baselines BASE-06–09 (scripted and random predator and prey), new EVAL claims
Goal 4 → REPLAY widened (generation gallery), SELF cross-play table
Goal 5 → CORE-01 teams, CORE-08 (predator-prey runs through the shared core)
```

### Assumptions (correct any now; otherwise I'll use these)
1. **World:**
   - the S2 tank (radius 1) with **1 predator and 6 prey**;
   - **8 pellets** at a time, each respawning at a random spot **20 steps** after it's eaten;
   - a prey eats a pellet within **0.05**, and the predator catches a prey within **0.08**;
   - **1,000-step** games.
2. **Speeds:** prey top speed **0.024** (S2's fish); predator **0.03** (S2's ball speed). The predator is faster, so the prey must use pellets and space wisely.
3. **Vision:** each prey sees the predator and its **3 nearest pellets** within **0.5**. The predator sees its **3 nearest prey** within **0.6**. Neither sees the whole tank.
4. **Rewards and scores:** prey get **+1 per pellet** and **−5 when caught**; the predator gets **+1 per catch**. The prey side's score is mean reward per prey per game, and the predator's is catches per game.
5. **The ladder:**
   - **rung 1** is two separate runs, prey against the scripted predator and the predator against scripted prey;
   - **rung 2** trains both sides at once against each other's latest policy;
   - **rung 3** samples each episode's opponent as **50% latest, 50% uniform over that side's saved past generations**.
   - All of them use your S2 PPO.
6. **Generations and cross-play:** **10 generations** per side per run. A **10×10 cross-play table** of 50 games per cell runs on held-out seeds.
7. **Scripted opponents:**
   - **predator:** swims at full speed toward the nearest visible prey, otherwise toward the tank centre;
   - **prey:** flee at full speed when the predator is within 0.3, otherwise swim to the nearest visible pellet.
   - Random players draw uniformly from the unit disc, as in S2.
8. **Budget:** ≤ 60 min laptop CPU per run, $5 cloud per stage, 5 training seeds, 200 held-out games per seed. That's the same as before.

### Questions (these change requirements)
1. **Rating.** Each generation's **mean score against the opponent side's generations** (default, simple and honest), or an **Elo-style rating** from cross-play games? Elo needs a definition of a "win", such as the predator catching at least 3 prey in a game.
2. **Caught prey.** Do they **respawn after 50 steps** at a random spot at least 0.4 from the predator (default, so games stay full and the predator keeps learning), or stay **out for the rest of the game**?
3. **The rung 3 pass bar.** Must **both sides'** rung 3 policies beat rung 2's against a fixed panel (default)? Or is **at least one side** enough? The panel is the scripted opponent, the random opponent and rung 1's trained opponent.
4. **The rung 2 failure.** My default: the spec **reports** whether rung 2 cycled or collapsed (shown in the cross-play table and labelled), but doesn't *require* it. A spec can't honestly require a failure. Is that right?

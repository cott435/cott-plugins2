### Residual target and features (`FEAT`)
**Serves:** Goal 1
**Stage:** S3
**Source:** [ideas/residual-return-features.md](ideas/residual-return-features.md)
**Behavior:** At every rebalance date R of the portfolio study (PORT-02), each eligible member (PORT-03) gets a target, its market-removed return over the following month, and 22 candidate features built only from its prices and volume through R. The features are profiled for coverage, distributions and correlation with each other, and the profile is part of the feature study's report.

**Requirements**
- **FEAT-01** Each eligible member's target for R is the sum of its daily residual returns over the month after R: each day's return with the part explained by the components above the noise edge (at least 1) removed, the components and the stock's exposure to them being those fitted on the 252 trading days ending R (STRUC-04).
  - Given a synthetic stock whose residual daily returns in the month after R are +1.0%, −0.5% and +0.2%, when its target is computed, then it is +0.7%.
  - Given a synthetic one-factor window where the month after R follows the window's factor model with no noise, when targets are computed, then every target is 0 within 1e-9.
  - Given a stock whose last trading day is the 10th trading day of the month after R, when its target is computed, then it sums the residual returns through day 10, that day included.
- **FEAT-02** Each eligible member has 13 base features for R, each computed from the 252 trading days ending R (prices adjusted as of R, RET-05):

  | Feature | Definition |
  |---|---|
  | Momentum 12-1 | Total return over the 252 days ending R, leaving out the last 21 |
  | Momentum 6-1 | Total return over the 126 days ending R, leaving out the last 21 |
  | 1-month reversal | Total return over the last 21 days |
  | 1-week reversal | Total return over the last 5 days |
  | Volatility 21 | Standard deviation of daily returns over the last 21 days |
  | Volatility 252 | Standard deviation of daily returns over the 252 days |
  | Beta | Slope of the stock's daily returns on the equal-weighted average daily return of R's eligible members, over the 252 days |
  | Maximum return | Largest daily return over the last 21 days |
  | Skewness | Skewness of daily returns over the 252 days |
  | Illiquidity | Mean of the absolute daily return divided by that day's dollar volume (close × volume), over the last 21 days |
  | Dollar volume | Log of the mean daily dollar volume over the last 21 days |
  | Volume trend | Mean daily volume over the last 21 days divided by the mean over the 252 days |
  | Distance from high | Close on R divided by the highest close over the 252 days |

  - Given a synthetic stock whose every feature is known by construction, when its features for R are computed, then each equals its constructed value within 1e-9.
  - Given a stock whose daily return is exactly 1.5 × the members' average every day of the window, when its beta is computed, then it is 1.5.
  - Given a stock with volume 2,000 on each of the last 21 days and 1,000 on each earlier day of the window, when its volume trend is computed, then it is 2,000 / ((21 × 2,000 + 231 × 1,000) / 252) = 1.846 to 3 decimals.
- **FEAT-03** Each of the 9 return-based features (both momentums, both reversals, both volatilities, maximum return, skewness, illiquidity) also has a residual version, computed the same way from the stock's residual daily returns in the window ending R (STRUC-04), with returns over a span summed rather than compounded, giving 22 candidate features in all.
  - Given a completed feature build, when the candidates for R are listed, then there are exactly 22: the 13 base features and 9 residual versions.
  - Given the one-factor synthetic window of STRUC-02, when the residual 1-month reversal is computed, then it equals the sum of the stock's residual returns over the last 21 days of the window.
- **FEAT-04** The features are profiled with the `data-analysis` skill on every rebalance date: coverage (the share of eligible members with a value), distribution summaries and tails, and the rank correlation between every pair of candidates. It passes when the report has all three for every candidate across all rebalance dates.
  - Given a completed study, when the profile part of its report is read, then each of the 22 candidates has its coverage by rebalance date, its distribution summary and its correlations with the other 21.
- **FEAT-05** Every feature for R, and the components and exposures its target uses, are built from no data after R; only the target's own month comes after R.
  - Given a price or volume changed on a day after R, when the study is run again, then every feature for R and earlier is identical.
  - Given a return changed on a day after the month that follows R, when the study is run again, then the target for R is identical.

**Edge cases & errors**
- A feature with no value for a stock on R (zero dollar volume, for example) → the value is left missing and counts against that feature's coverage; the stock still counts for every feature it has.
- A stock with no trading day after R → it has no target for R, is left out of every test for that month, and the number left out is reported.
- A stock-month missing any of the 22 candidates → left out of the combined models for that month, and the number left out is reported for every rebalance date.

### Feature information study (`INFO`)
**Serves:** Goal 1, Goal 3
**Stage:** S3
**Source:** [ideas/residual-return-features.md](ideas/residual-return-features.md)
**Behavior:** The researcher runs one command. It tests every candidate feature on its own, then a linear and a non-linear model that combine all 22, then each feature inside each model, always against a null in which the information is shuffled away. Everything is chosen on the earlier years; the final ~5 years are locked until the researcher freezes the settings and asks for them to be scored, once. The report gives one verdict per feature and the combined models against the best single feature, with controls showing whether the tests can be trusted. "Nothing survives" is a valid result. The report opens in the app.

**Requirements**
- **INFO-01** One command runs the whole study and writes one report with five parts: feature profile, per-feature verdicts, combined models, controls and holdout record.
  - Given stored data from 1998-01-02, when the command is run, then one report is written that has all five parts.
- **INFO-02** Rebalance dates before 2021-09-30 are the development months; rebalance dates from 2021-09-30 onward are the holdout, the final ~5 years at the time of writing. The boundary is fixed and does not move as data is added.
  - Given data through 2026-09-30, when the study runs, then the holdout is the 60 rebalance dates from 2021-09-30 to 2026-08-31.
  - Given a later refresh that adds a year of data, when the study runs, then the first holdout date is still 2021-09-30.
- **INFO-03** A development run uses no data from after 2021-09-30.
  - Given a price, volume or return changed on a day after 2021-09-30, when the development run is repeated, then every result in its report is identical.
- **INFO-04** The holdout is scored only when the researcher asks for it, after freezing the settings; the report records the date it was scored and the frozen settings, and any later scoring of the holdout is labelled "holdout reused".
  - Given a development run, when its report is read, then no holdout result is shown and the holdout record says "not yet scored".
  - Given the holdout scored on day H, when the report is read, then it states H and every setting used.
  - Given the holdout already scored, when it is scored again with any settings, then the new report is labelled "holdout reused" and still states the first scoring date.
- **INFO-05** The combined models are trained walk-forward: the first test month is the 61st rebalance date, and the models are refit every 12 rebalance dates on all earlier rebalance dates whose target month has ended by the refit date. Model settings are chosen on development months only; holdout months are predicted with the frozen settings, refit on the same schedule.
  - Given a refit on rebalance date R, when its training months are listed, then every one is earlier than R and its target month ended on or before R.
  - Given the first test month, when its training months are counted, then there are 60.
- **INFO-06** A prediction's score is the mean, over test months, of the monthly rank correlation across stocks between the predictions and the targets; the out-of-sample R² is reported next to it.
  - Given a month where the predictions rank the stocks exactly as their targets, when scored, then that month's rank correlation is 1.
  - Given a completed study, when the combined-models part is read, then every model and the baseline show both the score and the out-of-sample R².
- **INFO-07** Each candidate feature is tested on its own with the `null-hypothesis-testing` skill, given the feature's monthly rank correlations with the targets: the null is that the feature carries no information, with its distribution from shuffling the feature across stocks within each month, two-sided. It passes on the development months only if the null is rejected at 5% after Holm correction across the 22 candidates, and on the holdout only if it is rejected again, with the same sign, at 5% after Holm correction across the features that passed on development months.
  - Given a completed study, when the per-feature part is read, then each of the 22 candidates shows its mean rank correlation, its corrected p-value and whether it passed, for the development months and, once scored, the holdout.
- **INFO-08** The baseline is the single candidate feature with the highest training rank correlation in absolute value, used with its sign as the prediction, chosen again at every refit.
  - Given a refit where 1-month reversal has the training rank correlation of largest magnitude, −0.04, when the baseline predicts, then its prediction is minus that feature.
- **INFO-09** Two combined models, one linear and one non-linear, each predict the targets from all 22 candidates.
  - Given a completed study, when the combined-models part is read, then both models show their scores on the development test months and, once scored, the holdout.
- **INFO-10** Each combined model is tested with the `null-hypothesis-testing` skill against the same model retrained walk-forward on targets shuffled across stocks within each month: the null is that its score is no higher than under shuffled targets. It passes only if the null is rejected one-sided at 5% after Holm correction across the 2 models.
  - Given a completed study, when the combined-models part is read, then each model shows its corrected p-value against shuffled targets and whether the null is rejected.
- **INFO-11** Each combined model is tested with the `null-hypothesis-testing` skill against the baseline (INFO-08), on the paired monthly rank correlations: the null is that the model's score is no higher than the baseline's. It passes only if the null is rejected one-sided at 5% after Holm correction across the 2 models.
  - Given a completed study, when the combined-models part is read, then each model shows its corrected p-value against the baseline and whether the null is rejected.
- **INFO-12** Each feature's contribution to each combined model is tested with the `null-hypothesis-testing` skill: the feature is shuffled across stocks within each test month and the model's score recomputed, and the null is that the score does not fall. It passes only if the null is rejected one-sided at 5% after Holm correction across the 22 candidates within that model.
  - Given a completed study, when the per-feature part is read, then each candidate shows, for each model, its fall in score, its corrected p-value and whether it passed.
- **INFO-13** Each candidate gets one verdict, survives or not: it survives only if it passes INFO-07 on both the development months and the holdout. Until the holdout is scored, the verdict reads "development only".
  - Given a feature that passes INFO-07 on the development months but not on the holdout, when the verdict is read, then it does not survive.
  - Given a study where no feature survives, when the report is read, then it says so plainly, with every feature's figures still shown.
- **INFO-14** A planted feature is the required positive control: built from the targets plus independent noise so that its true rank correlation with them is 0.03, it is tested as in INFO-07, in the same Holm family, and must pass on the development months. It is never used by the combined models or the baseline.
  - Given a completed study, when the controls part is read, then the planted feature's rank correlation, corrected p-value and pass or fail are shown.
- **INFO-15** 100 pure-noise features, drawn independently of all data, are the required negative control: each is tested as in INFO-07 before correction, and between 1 and 10 of the 100 may be rejected at 5%. They are never used by the combined models or the baseline.
  - Given a completed study, when the controls part is read, then the number of noise features rejected out of 100 is shown with pass or fail.
- **INFO-16** If either required control fails, the report states at its top that the tests can't be trusted.
  - Given a study where the planted feature does not pass, when the report is opened, then its first line says the tests can't be trusted and names the failed control.
- **INFO-17** Whether 1-month reversal passes INFO-07 on real data is reported in the controls part as a known-effect check; its failing does not mark the tests untrustworthy.
  - Given a study where 1-month reversal does not pass, when the report is read, then the controls part says so and the report is not marked untrustworthy for it.
- **INFO-18** The feature study's report can be opened in the app.
  - Given a completed study, when the researcher opens it in the app, then its five parts' tables and charts are shown.
- **INFO-19** The full study, nulls included, runs in ≤ 4 hours.
  - Timed on the researcher's machine on the full history since 1998-01-02.

3. **FEAT-02:** beta is measured against the equal-weighted average return of that month's eligible members. The residual versions sum returns over a span instead of compounding them.
4. **INFO-14:** the planted control is tested in the same Holm family as the 22 candidates and is never given to the models.
5. Numbers I set:
   - a month with fewer than 30 stocks that have both a target and a feature gives no rank correlation for that feature
   - a stock-month missing any candidate is left out of the models, and the count is reported
   - a refit where a model fails drops those months from both sides of every comparison, so they stay paired

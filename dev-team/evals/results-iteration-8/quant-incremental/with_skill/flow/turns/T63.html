### AD-18: Constrained optimisation with cvxpy
**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Scope:** `qrisk`
- **Context:** PORT-01–PORT-03 need long-only, fully invested portfolios with a 5% cap on up to ~500 names, about 2,250 solves per run within QR-12's 30 minutes, and minimum variance exact to 1e-6.
- **Decision:** cvxpy with the Clarabel solver. Minimum variance is a quadratic programme with box and budget constraints. Risk parity is the convex log-barrier formulation (minimise ½wᵀΣw − Σ log wᵢ, then normalise); names that breach the cap are fixed at it and the rest re-solved until none breach.
- **Alternatives:** scipy's SLSQP — slow and loose at 500 variables. riskfolio-lib — wraps cvxpy but hides the formulations this stage exists to learn.
- **Consequences:** The formulations are visible and tested against SYNTH and PORT's closed-form cases. A solver failure is a recorded `failed` portfolio (PORT-06).

It hands the `null-hypothesis-testing` skill two score sets and a direction (for example "dqn-compact greater than random", "ppo-1 greater than reinforce-1" or "rung-3 prey greater than rung-2 prey against the prey panel"). The skill chooses the test statistic, the null distribution and the multiple-comparison correction, and `compare` records the skill's verdict as a `compare.Claim`. `compare` adds no test procedure of its own. 61	## Data
62	- All data comes from the lab's own environments (DATA-02). No downloads.
63	- **Seeds (DATA-01):** a run's training seed `s` must be below 1,000,000. Its evaluation seeds are `1_000_000 + 1000·s + i`, a disjoint range. `evaluate.eval_seeds` asserts that none of them overlaps the run's training seed stream.

66	## Cross-cutting concerns
67	- **Reproducibility (RUN-03):** one seed feeds a single `np.random.Generator` and `torch.manual_seed`, passed down explicitly. PyTorch runs with one thread and deterministic algorithms (AD-08). No global random state.
68	- **Timing (QR-01–QR-04, QR-07):** predator-prey runs count both learning teams' updates inside one run's 60 minutes. Each run's progress lines and `curve.csv` carry elapsed seconds, so the limits are read from the runs themselves. A timing test trains each agent for 10,000 steps and extrapolates to its full step budget.

### AD-21: Residual returns come from the window's own components, fitted by least squares on the stocks trading each day
**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Scope:** `features`
- **Context:** FEAT-01 sums each member's residual daily returns over the month after R, with the components and exposures fitted on the window (STRUC-04), through the stock's last trading day. FEAT-03 builds residual features from the window's residuals, with spans summed. AD-17 projects the kept components out of standardised, demeaned returns for STRUC-04's correlations. Demeaning over the window would make the residual momentum 12-1 equal to minus the last 21 days' sum.
- **Decision:** From `eqdata_study.spectrum(window)`, take σ = `sd` and V, the first `kept` eigenvectors. On day t, in the window or in the month after R, let A be the window's stocks with a return that day, and z = x / σ on A. The factor returns are f = (V_Aᵀ V_A)⁻¹ V_Aᵀ z_A, and stock i's residual is x_i − σ_i (V f)_i. No mean is removed. With every stock present, V_Aᵀ V_A = I and f = Vᵀ z, AD-17's projection. The target is the sum of the month's residuals through the last trading day, and NaN with none.
- **Alternatives:** STRUC-04's demeaned standardised residuals: residual momentum would mirror residual reversal. Missing stocks counted as 0 in f: biased factor returns on every day a member has stopped trading. Per-stock time-series betas on the factor returns: a second fit per stock for the same projection.
- **Consequences:** FEAT-01's noiseless one-factor month gives 0 within 1e-9. One k-column least-squares solve per day, ~21 a month. `Residuals` carries the window's and the month's residuals to `build` and `target`.

### AD-22: A ridge model and histogram gradient boosting on scikit-learn, trained walk-forward
**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Scope:** `features`
- **Context:** UR-90: "Your default: linear baseline (rank correlation plus ridge), then gradient boosting, walk-forward." INFO-05, INFO-06, INFO-08, INFO-09 and INFO-19 (4 hours, nulls included). Out of scope: deep learning, and any search over settings that uses the holdout months. The rank-correlation half of the linear baseline is INFO-07 and INFO-08. About 135,000 complete stock-months × 22 at the last refit.
- **Decision:** scikit-learn ≥ 1.5. Inputs: each candidate ranked across stocks within its month and scaled to [−0.5, 0.5], ties averaged; the target as is. The refits follow INFO-05. **Ridge** (`Ridge`): at each refit, α is picked from the settings' grid by score on the refit's last 12 training months, fitted on the earlier ones, then refit on all its training months. **Gradient boosting** (`HistGradientBoostingRegressor`, squared error): the frozen settings, with the iteration count found by early stopping on the same 12 months, then refit on all training months. Its `random_state` comes from the settings' seed. **Baseline:** INFO-08, with its R² from a one-variable least-squares fit of the target on the signed ranked feature over the training months. **Score:** the mean monthly rank correlation (INFO-06), with out-of-sample R² = 1 − Σ(y − ŷ)² / Σy², the zero-forecast benchmark. A fit that raises or predicts a non-finite value is a failure for its refit's months (INFO edge case). The frozen settings are the grid, the boosting settings and this procedure. At a holdout refit, they choose α and the iteration count on that refit's training months.
- **Alternatives:** LightGBM: faster, but a second native dependency. It replaces `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` if INFO-19's benchmark fails. XGBoost: the same trade. `GradientBoostingRegressor`: exact splits, ~10× slower at 135,000 rows. Settings tuned on the development test months: a search on the months the score is reported on.
- **Consequences:** One library for both models and the baseline's fit. Neither model sees the planted or noise controls (INFO-14, INFO-15). The 12-month inner block leaves the first refit 48 months for its α and iteration-count fits.

### AD-23: Every null shuffles across stocks within a month, from one seeded generator
**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Scope:** `features`
- **Context:** INFO-07, INFO-10–INFO-12 and INFO-14–INFO-17. UR-86: "Every feature claim has to pass a proper null-hypothesis test so we don't fool ourselves." UR-104 fixes two-sided per-feature tests and one-sided model tests, and puts the planted control in the candidates' Holm family. Holm's smallest threshold is 0.05 / 23 ≈ 0.0022 for the per-feature family, 0.05 / 22 for contributions and 0.025 for the 2 models. INFO-19 allows 4 hours.
- **Decision:** One `numpy.random.Generator` seeded from the settings' `seed`, with one child stream per test and control (`SeedSequence.spawn`), so adding a test moves no other draw. **Per feature:** the monthly rank correlation over months with at least 30 stocks; the null shuffles the feature across stocks within each month, with `permutations` ≥ 999 draws. **Planted control:** z is the normal scores of the month's target ranks, then ρ z + √(1 − ρ²) ε with ρ = 2 sin(π · 0.03 / 6) ≈ 0.0314, because a bivariate normal's rank correlation is (6/π) arcsin(ρ/2). **Noise:** 100 independent standard-normal features per stock-month, tested before correction. **Models against shuffled targets:** `model_permutations` ≥ 99 full walk-forward retrains per model on targets shuffled within each month, run in `workers` processes. **Models against the baseline:** paired monthly rank correlations on the months both predicted. **Contributions:** each feature shuffled within each test month, `permutations` times, through the fitted refit models, without retraining. The `null-hypothesis-testing` skill picks each test's statistic and p-value computation within these nulls, and may raise the counts.
- **Alternatives:** 99 draws per feature: the smallest p-value, 0.01, can never pass Holm's 0.0022. Retraining for each contribution shuffle: 22 × 2 × 999 walk-forward fits for a question re-prediction answers. Analytic normal approximations alone: left to the skill, which may use one only where it checks it.
- **Consequences:** The shuffled-target retrains dominate the run: ~23 refits × 99 × 2 models, of which ~2,300 are boosting fits. A rerun with the same settings reproduces every p-value.

# eqdata — Architecture

**Package:** eqdata · **Status:** Approved · **Last updated:** 2026-10-18 · **System:** [../../architecture.md](../../architecture.md)

## Overview
`sources` fetches from Sharadar and Yahoo. `sync` cleans what Sharadar returns, stages it and commits it to `store` in one transaction, then calls `check`. `universe` and `returns` answer as-of queries from `store`. `check` and `eda` raise findings through `findings`. Every long job runs inside `runs`, which holds the lock and writes progress events. `cli` is the front door. The separate `server` package calls only the public API in [contract.md](contract.md): the query functions, `launch_run`, which spawns the CLI (AD-09), and `run_snapshot` and `events_after` for live progress (AD-13). The separate `study` and `features` packages read the as-of loader, and `features` also the daily bars, through the same API. Both save their reports through `reports`, which also holds the EDA report and serves every kind to `server` (AD-14, AD-15, AD-19).

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  cli --> runs & sync & check & eda
  runs --> store
  sync --> sources & store & check
  sync & check & eda -.Progress.-> runs
  check --> sources & universe & returns & findings & calendar & store
  eda --> universe & returns & findings & reports
  views --> returns & universe & findings
  universe --> store & calendar
  returns --> store & calendar
  findings --> store
```

## Data
- **Sources:** Sharadar SEP, ACTIONS, TICKERS and SP500 (AD-03). Yahoo via `yahooquery` for the sample only (AD-05). Researcher CSVs under `reference/` (AD-10).
- **Tables** (all keyed by `company_id`, AD-04): `companies`, `tickers` (company, ticker, from, to), `membership` (company, effective_from, effective_to), `prices` (unadjusted OHLCV), `actions` (splits, dividends, ticker changes, mergers, acquisitions, delistings). Each has `known_from` and `replaced_on` (AD-06). Also `findings`, `runs`, `run_events`, `source_diffs` (run, company, date, return and close differences; AD-12), and staging copies of the market tables.
- **Cleaning (SYNC-03):** duplicate (company, date) rows keep one, and the count goes to the summary. A changed past value closes the old row (`replaced_on`) and inserts the new one, listed as a revision.
- **Known-on queries (DATA-05):** as-of functions take `as_of` (market date: events after it are ignored) and an optional `known_on` (system date: rows not yet known, or already replaced, then are ignored).
- **Sampling (CHECK-01):** at least 2 companies per calendar year from 1998, drawn from members Yahoo still carries, then filled at random to 100. The seed is recorded in the run summary.
- **Reports:** one `report.json` (an `eqdata.Report`: sections of tables and chart series) per report in `EQDATA_HOME/reports/<kind>/<id>/`, for the `eda`, `study` and `features` kinds, rendered by `web` (AD-15, AD-24).

## Cross-cutting concerns
- **Lock and atomicity:** `runs` holds the lock and `store.commit` is one transaction, so a killed refresh leaves only staging rows, which are cleared at the next start.
- **Credentials:** read in `sources` only, and redacted in logs and events (QR-05).

```python
def run_features(*, known_on: date | None = None, score_holdout: bool = False, settings_path: Path | None = None) -> Report:
    today = date.today()
    phase: Phase = "holdout" if score_holdout else "development"
    settings = load_settings(settings_path or SETTINGS_PATH)
    inputs = load_inputs(known_on=known_on or today, phase=phase)
    months = run_months(inputs)
    profiled = profile(months)
    controls = make_controls(months, settings)
    tests, outcome = run_single_tests(months, controls, settings, phase=phase)
    schedule = refits(inputs.panel.rebalances)
    predictions = run_models(months, schedule, settings)
    scores, model_tests, contributions = run_model_tests(months, schedule, predictions, settings, phase=phase)
    record = holdout_record(settings, phase=phase, today=today)
    sections = report_sections(months, profiled, tests, outcome, scores, model_tests, contributions, record)
    params = report_params(inputs, record, settings)
    return save_report("features", sections, params=params, notice=report_notice(outcome))
```

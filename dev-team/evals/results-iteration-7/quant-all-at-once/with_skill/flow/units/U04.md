## Recommendation
Sharadar Prices Full History, on its own. Before relying on it:
- Use the 3-week window to download everything in bulk.
- Check coverage: join the `sp500` deletions to the price table and confirm at least 95% of former members have prices up to their delisting date.
- Build dividend-adjusted open/high/low prices yourself from `closeadj` divided by `close`.

**Fallback (about $20/mo):** EODHD Historian ($199/yr) for prices of delisted stocks and the ticker-rename endpoint, plus fja05680/sp500 and Wikipedia for membership. Over budget but the most robust option: Norgate Platinum ($52.50/mo) in a Windows virtual machine.

# Quant Lab — Spec

**Status:** Draft · **Last updated:** 2026-10-17 · **Brief:** [brief.md](brief.md)

## Summary
A local, trustworthy daily US stock dataset (prices, returns, corporate actions and a universe matching each past date, including delisted companies) built from dated download snapshots, validated against a second source, and served through a loader that never returns information from the future. A browser app lets the single user explore the data, inspect every validation result, and start and watch build runs live. See the [brief](brief.md) for why.

## Users
| User | What they need to do |
|---|---|
| The researcher (sole user) | Build and update the dataset, load point-in-time data for research, inspect why the data can be trusted, and explore stocks, dates and data problems in the app |

## Scope
**In scope:** Universe by date (`UNIV`), Prices & corporate actions (`PRICE`), Returns (`RET`), As-of loader (`LOAD`), Data build (`BUILD`), Validation & flags (`VAL`), Exploratory analysis (`EDA`), Explorer app (`APP`), Run monitor (`RUN`).

**Out of scope:**
- Intraday data, options, futures and crypto.
- Live trading, real money and any brokerage connection.
- Hosting the app or serving any user other than the researcher; logins.
- Selling or publishing signals.
- Signal research, backtests and paper trading (later stages, not yet planned).
- Company fundamentals, earnings, news and analyst data.
- Scheduled or automatic updates; every run is started by hand.
- Real-time or same-day quotes.

## Features

### Universe by date (`UNIV`)
**Serves:** Goal 1
**Behavior:** For any trading date in the history, the researcher gets the stocks that were S&P 500 members on that date, including members that were later delisted, acquired or went bankrupt. Every stock has a permanent identity independent of its ticker.

**Requirements**
- **UNIV-01** For any trading date, the system returns the stocks that were index members on that date.
  - Given a stock that joined the index effective date J and left effective date L, when members on date D are requested, then the stock is included if and only if J ≤ D < L.
  - Given a member that went bankrupt and was removed in 2009, when members on 2008-06-30 are requested, then it is included.
- **UNIV-02** The member count on every trading date lies within a plausible range.
  - Given any trading date in the history, when members are counted, then the count is between 490 and 510; any date outside that range fails validation (`VAL-08`).
- **UNIV-03** Every membership change records which kind of date it carries.
  - Given a membership change, when it is inspected, then it shows its effective date, its announcement date if known, and whether the announcement date is known or missing.
- **UNIV-04** Each stock has a permanent identity that never changes, whatever its ticker.
  - Given a company that changed ticker, when its history is loaded by its permanent identity, then one continuous history spanning both tickers is returned.
- **UNIV-05** A ticker resolves to the company that held it on the requested date.
  - Given a ticker used by company A until 2015 and reused by company B from 2018, when the ticker is looked up on 2012-03-01, then company A is returned; on 2020-03-02, company B.
  - Given a ticker looked up on a date no company held it, then the lookup reports "no stock held this ticker on this date".

**Edge cases & errors**
- Two share classes of one company both in the index → each is a separate member with its own identity.
- A stock added and removed several times → each membership period is kept; UNIV-01 applies per period.
- A membership change dated on a non-trading day → it takes effect on the next trading day.

### Prices & corporate actions (`PRICE`)
**Serves:** Goal 1, Goal 2
**Behavior:** Every member has daily raw open, high, low, close and volume for each trading day it was listed during membership, plus its splits, cash dividends and delisting. Adjusted prices are derived from raw prices and the corporate action records, so every adjustment can be traced.

**Requirements**
- **PRICE-01** The dataset holds daily raw open, high, low, close and volume for every member on every trading day it was a listed member.
  - Given the full dataset, when member-days are counted, then a raw close exists for ≥ 99.5% of them; each missing member-day is flagged (`VAL-05`).
- **PRICE-02** Each split is recorded with its date and ratio.
  - Given the hand-checked list of known corporate actions (`VAL-07`), when the dataset is built, then every listed split is present with the listed date and ratio.
- **PRICE-03** Each cash dividend is recorded with its ex-date and amount per share.
  - Given the hand-checked list of known corporate actions, when the dataset is built, then every listed dividend is present with the listed ex-date and an amount within $0.0001 of the listed amount.
- **PRICE-04** Adjusted prices are derived from raw prices and recorded corporate actions only.
  - Given a 4-for-1 split effective on day S, when adjusted closes are read, then the adjusted close on the trading day before S equals that day's raw close divided by 4 (to within $0.0001, before any dividend adjustment).
  - Given a cash dividend with ex-date X, when adjusted closes are read, then every adjusted close before X is scaled by (1 − dividend ÷ raw close on the day before X).
- **PRICE-05** Every adjusted price can be traced to its raw price and the corporate actions applied.
  - Given any stock and date, when the trace is requested, then the raw price, each corporate action applied (type, date, factor) and the resulting adjusted price are shown, and multiplying the raw price by the listed factors reproduces the adjusted price.
- **PRICE-06** Each delisting is recorded with its date and reason category.
  - Given a delisted stock, when it is inspected, then its last trading date and a reason of merger, bankruptcy or liquidation, other, or unknown are shown.

**Edge cases & errors**
- Reverse split (e.g. 1-for-10) → handled by the same rules as a forward split, with ratio < 1.
- Special or stock dividends → recorded as their own action type; a type the system can't adjust for is flagged, not ignored.
- Raw prices and actions that disagree (a raw price halves with no split recorded) → caught by `VAL-03`.

### Returns (`RET`)
**Serves:** Goal 1
**Behavior:** Every stock has a daily total return (price change plus dividends, corrected for splits) and a price-only return, with the delisting return included when it is known.

**Requirements**
- **RET-01** Daily total return includes dividends paid.
  - Given a day that is a stock's ex-dividend date, when its total return is read, then it equals (close + dividend) ÷ previous close − 1, with both closes on the same split basis.
- **RET-02** Splits never appear as returns.
  - Given a 4-for-1 split day with an unchanged company value, when its total return is read, then it is within ±1% of 0, not about −75%.
- **RET-03** A daily price-only return excluding dividends is available.
  - Given an ex-dividend day, when its price-only return is read, then it equals close ÷ previous close − 1 on the same split basis.
- **RET-04** A delisted stock's final return includes the delisting payout when known.
  - Given a stock acquired for cash at $50 with a last close of $49, when its final return is read, then it reflects the $50 payout.
  - Given a delisted stock whose delisting payout is unknown, when its final return is read, then it is marked "delisting return unknown" and is never filled with an estimate.
- **RET-05** A return spanning missing days is marked as a multi-day return.
  - Given a stock with no price on two consecutive trading days, when the next return is read, then it is marked as covering 3 trading days.

**Edge cases & errors**
- First trading day of a stock → no return for that day.
- Zero or negative raw close → the row is rejected by `VAL-02`, and returns touching it are absent and flagged.

### As-of loader (`LOAD`)
**Serves:** Goal 1, Goal 3, Goal 5
**Behavior:** The researcher asks for a date range and gets the universe, prices and returns as they were known on each date, from a named dataset version. Nothing published after a date is ever visible for that date.

**Requirements**
- **LOAD-01** For each date in a request, the loader returns only information known on that date.
  - Given a stock whose index addition was announced on date A and took effect on date E, when the universe for any date before A is loaded, then the stock is absent.
  - Given a request for dates up to D, when the result is inspected, then no value whose known-date is after D appears.
- **LOAD-02** The loader includes delisted stocks for the dates they were members.
  - Given a range covering a member that was delisted during the range, when loaded, then its data appears up to and including its last trading date.
- **LOAD-03** Every loader result names the dataset version it came from.
  - Given any load, when the result is inspected, then it carries the dataset's fingerprint.
- **LOAD-04** Loading the same request from the same dataset version always returns identical data.
  - Given a request run today and again a month later against the same fingerprint, when the two results are compared, then they are identical.
- **LOAD-05** Rejected rows are never returned; flagged rows are returned with their flags.
  - Given a request covering a flagged row, when loaded, then the row appears with its flag and reason; with "exclude flagged" set, it is absent.
- **LOAD-06** By default the loader refuses a dataset that failed validation.
  - Given the newest dataset failed a dataset-level check (`VAL-08`), when a load is requested without naming a version, then the newest passing dataset is used and the result says so.
  - Given the researcher names the failed dataset explicitly, when loaded, then it loads with a warning.

**Edge cases & errors**
- A range outside the history → the loader returns what exists and reports the uncovered dates.
- No passing dataset exists → the load fails with a message saying so.
- A ticker instead of a permanent identity → resolved per `UNIV-05` for each date.

### Data build (`BUILD`)
**Serves:** Goal 3
**Behavior:** A full build downloads the whole history, saves it as a dated raw snapshot, then cleans, validates and stores a new dataset version. A manual update fetches new days, saves a new snapshot and reports what changed. Builds start from the app or the command line.

**Requirements**
- **BUILD-01** A full build produces a validated dataset version from fresh downloads.
  - Given no existing data, when a full build is started, then a dated raw snapshot is saved, followed by a dataset version with a fingerprint, a validation report and an exploratory report.
- **BUILD-02** A manual update adds the trading days since the last build.
  - Given a dataset ending on date T, when an update is started, then the new dataset version covers through the latest completed trading day.
- **BUILD-03** Each update reports what changed from the previous version.
  - Given a source revised a 2015 price since the last snapshot, when an update runs, then the change list shows that stock, date, old value and new value.
- **BUILD-04** Rebuilding from the same raw snapshots gives an identical dataset.
  - Given a set of saved snapshots, when the dataset is rebuilt from them twice, on different days, then both fingerprints are equal.
- **BUILD-05** Saved raw snapshots are never changed.
  - Given a snapshot whose contents were altered after saving, when a rebuild reads it, then the rebuild stops and names the altered snapshot.
- **BUILD-06** A failed or cancelled run leaves the previous dataset untouched.
  - Given a run stopped halfway, when the loader is used, then it returns the previous dataset version, with an unchanged fingerprint.
- **BUILD-07** Only one run can be active at a time.
  - Given a run in progress, when another is started from the app or command line, then it is refused with a message naming the active run.
- **BUILD-08** Source failures are retried, then reported precisely.
  - Given a source that fails 3 times in a row for one request, when the run gives up, then it fails with a message naming the source, the request and the data missing, and no new dataset version is stored.
- **BUILD-09** The command line and the app start the same runs.
  - Given a full build started from the command line and another from the app on the same snapshots, when both finish, then their fingerprints are equal.

**Edge cases & errors**
- Update started on a non-trading day or before the close → it covers through the last completed trading day.
- Nothing new since the last build → the run finishes with "no new data" and stores no new version.
- Disk space below the needed amount → the run fails before downloading, with the amount needed.

### Validation & flags (`VAL`)
**Serves:** Goal 2
**Behavior:** Every build runs a named set of checks. Each failing row is rejected, corrected or kept with a flag, always with a reason. Dataset-level checks decide whether the version passes. A validation report summarises everything and links down to individual rows.

**Requirements**
- **VAL-01** Every failing row gets exactly one outcome with a recorded reason.
  - Given a row that fails a check, when it is inspected, then it shows the outcome (rejected, corrected or kept with a flag), the check name, a plain-English reason, and for corrections the original value.
- **VAL-02** Rows with impossible prices are rejected.
  - Given a row with a close ≤ 0, or a high below the low, or a close outside the high-low range, when validated, then it is rejected.
- **VAL-03** Large moves without a matching corporate action are flagged.
  - Given a daily price-only return beyond ±50% with no split or special dividend that day, when validated, then the row is flagged "large move with no corporate action".
- **VAL-04** Stale prices are flagged.
  - Given a close unchanged for 5 or more consecutive trading days, when validated, then those days are flagged "stale price".
- **VAL-05** Missing member-days and duplicate rows are caught.
  - Given a member with no price on a trading day, when validated, then that member-day is flagged "missing".
  - Given two rows for the same stock and date, when validated, then both are rejected unless identical, in which case one is kept and the other is recorded as a removed duplicate.
- **VAL-06** Closing prices are cross-checked against a second independent source.
  - Given stock-days covered by both sources, when compared on raw close, then every disagreement over 0.5% is flagged, and the dataset-level check passes only if ≥ 99.5% of those stock-days agree.
  - Given a stock with disagreements on more than 1% of its overlapping days, when validated, then the whole stock is flagged.
- **VAL-07** Corporate actions are checked against a hand-checked list of known events.
  - Given a list of at least 20 known splits and dividends, prepared outside the system, when a build is validated, then the dataset-level check passes only if every event is present with the right date and ratio or amount.
- **VAL-08** A dataset version that fails any dataset-level check is stored but marked as failed.
  - Given a build where `UNIV-02`, `VAL-06`, `VAL-07` or the history check (`DATA-01`) fails, when it finishes, then the version is marked "failed validation" with the failing checks listed.
- **VAL-09** Each build produces a validation report.
  - Given a finished build, when its report is opened, then it shows per check: rows checked, rows rejected, corrected and flagged; per stock: flag counts; and pass or fail for each dataset-level check.

**Edge cases & errors**
- A check that itself errors → the build fails and names the check; a broken check never counts as a pass.
- Second source missing a stock entirely → reported as "not cross-checked", counted separately from agreements and disagreements.

### Exploratory analysis (`EDA`)
**Serves:** Goal 2, Goal 4
**Behavior:** Each dataset version gets an exploratory report describing the data, so problems and features are visible before any research uses them.

**Requirements**
- **EDA-01** Each dataset version gets an exploratory report made with the `data-analysis` skill.
  - Given a dataset version, when the `data-analysis` skill is run on its daily total returns, prices and membership, then the report covers: summary statistics of returns overall and by year; distributions and tails; missingness by stock and by date; cross-sectional correlation structure across rolling windows; universe size and turnover over time. It is complete only when every section is present and each notable finding names the stocks and dates involved.
- **EDA-02** The report shows the size of survivorship bias in this data.
  - Given the point-in-time universe and a "today's members only" universe over the same history, when compared, then the report shows the equal-weight average annual return of each and the difference between them.
- **EDA-03** Each report is tied to the dataset version it describes.
  - Given a report, when opened, then it shows the dataset fingerprint; a report never describes a version other than the one named.

**Edge cases & errors**
- Dataset failed validation → the report is still made and opens with that warning.

### Explorer app (`APP`)
**Serves:** Goal 2, Goal 4
**Behavior:** A browser app, running locally for the researcher only, with views for a single stock, the universe on a date, validation results, the exploratory report and dataset versions. It is deliberately designed, not a dashboard template.

**Requirements**
- **APP-01** The stock view shows raw and adjusted prices with corporate actions and flags marked.
  - Given a stock, when its view opens, then a chart shows raw and adjusted close, a marker for each split, dividend and delisting, and a marker on each flagged day.
- **APP-02** Stocks can be found by ticker on a date or by name.
  - Given a reused ticker, when searched, then each company that held it is listed with the dates it held it.
- **APP-03** The universe view shows members on a chosen date.
  - Given a date, when the view opens, then it lists the members, the member count, and the joins and leaves in the 30 days before and after.
- **APP-04** The validation view drills from summary to rows.
  - Given a validation report, when the researcher picks a check, then the stocks it affected are listed; picking a stock lists its affected rows with outcome, reason and original values.
- **APP-05** Any adjusted price in the app can be traced.
  - Given a point on an adjusted-price chart, when the researcher asks for its trace, then the `PRICE-05` trace is shown.
- **APP-06** The exploratory report is viewable in the app.
  - Given a dataset version, when its report is opened, then every `EDA-01` section is shown with its charts.
- **APP-07** The researcher chooses which dataset version the app shows.
  - Given two versions, when one is chosen, then every view shows that version's fingerprint; when both are chosen for comparison, the `BUILD-03` change list between them is shown.
- **APP-08** The interface follows a design direction made with the `frontend-design` skill.
  - Given the `frontend-design` skill, when the design direction (typography, colour, layout, character) is written, then the researcher approves it before any view is built, and every view follows it.
- **APP-09** Every chart follows the `dataviz` skill's rules.
  - Given any chart in the app, when reviewed against the `dataviz` skill's checklist, then it passes, in both light and dark themes.

**Edge cases & errors**
- No dataset built yet → the app opens on a "build your first dataset" screen with a button to start a full build.
- Backend not running → the app says so and how to start it, rather than showing empty views.
- A stock with no data in the chosen version → the view says so and names the version.

### Run monitor (`RUN`)
**Serves:** Goal 4, Goal 3
**Behavior:** The researcher starts a full build or an update from the app and watches it live. Runs started from the command line appear in the app too.

**Requirements**
- **RUN-01** Full builds and updates can be started from the app.
  - Given no active run, when the researcher starts an update in the app, then a run begins and its live view opens.
- **RUN-02** A running run shows live progress.
  - Given an active run, when its view is open, then it shows the current step, percent complete, the current stock or source request, elapsed time and counts of rows rejected, corrected and flagged so far.
- **RUN-03** Runs started from the command line are visible live in the app.
  - Given a run started from the command line, when the app is open, then it appears and shows the same live progress.
- **RUN-04** A run can be cancelled from the app.
  - Given an active run, when the researcher cancels it, then it stops and `BUILD-06` holds.
- **RUN-05** A finished run shows a summary.
  - Given a finished run, when its view is open, then it shows success or failure, the failing step and error if any, the new fingerprint, and links to its validation report, exploratory report and change list.
- **RUN-06** Reopening the app mid-run restores the live view.
  - Given an active run, when the app is reloaded, then the live view shows the current state.
- **RUN-07** Past runs are listed.
  - Given previous runs, when the run history opens, then each run shows its start time, type, how it was started (app or command line), outcome and duration.

**Edge cases & errors**
- App closed during a run → the run continues; reopening shows it per `RUN-06`.
- Connection to the backend drops mid-run → the app shows "reconnecting" and resumes the live view when the connection returns.

## Data requirements
- **DATA-01** Daily data from 2006-01-01 (target) to the latest completed trading day, and never starting later than 2011-01-01.
  - Given a built dataset, when its first date is checked, then it is on or before 2011-01-01, or the build fails validation (`VAL-08`); the report states whether the 2006-01-01 target was met.
- **DATA-02** Coverage is every S&P 500 member on each date in the history, including members later delisted, acquired or bankrupt.
  - Given a list of at least 10 known former members that were delisted, prepared outside the system, when the dataset is built, then each appears with prices for its membership period.
- **DATA-03** Point-in-time membership history with an effective date for every change, and an announcement date where the source has one.
  - Given any membership change, when inspected, then its effective date is present.
- **DATA-04** Splits, cash dividends and delistings for every member, with delisting payouts where the source has them.
  - Given a delisting, when inspected, then a reason category is present, and a payout is present or marked unknown.
- **DATA-05** A permanent identity and dated ticker history for every stock.
  - Given any stock, when inspected, then every ticker it used is listed with its date range.
- **DATA-06** A second independent source of daily raw closing prices for cross-checking.
  - Given the full dataset, when overlap is measured, then the second source covers ≥ 80% of member-days since 2011-01-01.
- **DATA-07** The trading calendar matches actual US exchange trading days, including unscheduled closures.
  - Given 2012-10-29, 2012-10-30, 2018-12-05 and 2025-01-09, when the calendar is checked, then none is a trading day.
- **DATA-08** All data sources together cost no more than $50 a month and allow storing the data locally for personal research.
  - Checked against each source's price and terms before it is adopted.

## Quality requirements
- **QR-01** Loading any date range for the whole universe takes ≤ 5 seconds.
  - Timed on the researcher's laptop for the full history, all members, total returns.
- **QR-02** Every app view responds in ≤ 1 second once the data is built.
  - Timed from request to rendered view for each view on the full dataset.
- **QR-03** Run progress appears in the app ≤ 2 seconds after it happens.
  - Measured as the lag between a progress event and its display, during a full build.
- **QR-04** The full dataset, including all kept raw snapshots for one year of updates, uses ≤ 20 GB of disk.
  - Measured after a full build plus 12 monthly updates' worth of snapshots.
- **QR-05** The app and backend are reachable from the researcher's machine only.
  - A connection attempt from another device on the same network is refused.
- **QR-06** The app and backend share one contract definition, and any mismatch is caught before either runs.
  - Changing a field in the contract on one side only makes the project's checks fail.

## Assumptions
- The universe is the S&P 500 with membership as of each date; a wider universe is welcome if affordable data covers it, but is not required.
- A day's closing price is known at that day's close.
- Membership changes are known on their announcement date when the source has it, otherwise on their effective date, and each record says which.
- Reproducibility means identical datasets from identical saved snapshots; a fresh download may legitimately differ, and the differences are reported.
- The app has no login because only the researcher's machine can reach it.

## Open questions
- Whether affordable data covers all listed US stocks or only the S&P 500 by date — answered when data sources are compared in the architecture.
- Whether a second source reaching the `DATA-06` 80% overlap exists within budget — answered in the architecture; if not, the researcher decides whether to lower the overlap or the budget.

Given the hand-checked list of known corporate actions (`VAL-07`), when the dataset is built, then every listed split is present with the listed date and ratio.\n- **PRICE-03** Each cash dividend is recorded with its ex-date and amount per share.\n  - Given the hand-checked list of known corporate actions, when the dataset is built, then every listed dividend is present with the listed ex-date and an amount within $0.0001 of the listed amount.\n- **PRICE-04** Adjusted prices are derived from raw prices and recorded corporate actions only.\n  - Given a 4-for-1 split effective on day S, when adjusted closes are read, then the adjusted close on the trading day before S equals that day's raw close divided by 4 (to within $0.0001, before any dividend adjustment).\n  - Given a cash dividend with ex-date X, when adjusted closes are read, then every adjusted close before X is scaled by (1 − dividend ÷ raw close on the day before X).\n- **PRICE-05** Every adjusted price can be traced to its raw price and the corporate actions applied.\n  - Given a day that is a stock's ex-dividend date, when its total return is read, then it equals (close + dividend) ÷ previous close − 1, with both closes on the same split basis.\n- **RET-02** Splits never appear as returns.\n  - Given a 4-for-1 split day with an unchanged company value, when its total return is read, then it is within ±1% of 0, not about −75%.\n- **RET-03** A daily price-only return excluding dividends is available.\n  - Given an ex-dividend day, when its price-only return is read, then it equals close ÷ previous close − 1 on the same split basis.\n- **RET-04** A delisted stock's final return includes the delisting payout when known.\n  - Given a stock with no price on two consecutive trading days, when the next return is read, then it is marked as covering 3 trading days.\n\n**Edge cases & errors**\n- First trading day of a stock → no return for that day.\n- Zero or negative raw close → the row is rejected by `VAL-02`, and returns touching it are absent and flagged.\n\n### As-of loader (`LOAD`)\n**Serves:** Goal 1, Goal 3, Goal 5\n**Behavior:** The researcher asks for a date range and gets the universe, prices and returns as they were known on each date, from a named dataset version.

1	# Run
2	
3	Read when `dt build status` shows no build for the active milestone. It starts one and drives it
4	until a stop or the milestone exit. The loop is `loop.md`; stops are `escalation.md`.
5	
6	```
7	CHECK → [the user's go] → dt build start M<n> → loop.md → stop (escalation.md) or Exit
8	```
9	
10	## Steps
11	
12	**1. Check, then ask.** `dt status` shows the plan `Approved` with its `**Base:**` set, and
13	`dt status --skill build-setup` says `current`; the working tree is clean. Then ask for the go in
14	one message: the milestone, its task and half counts, the critical path and widest wave from
15	`dt status`, the concurrency cap (default 6), each `**Risk:** external` task (it waits for its own
16	yes), and that the build runs on its own from here until a stop or the exit. **Stop the turn.**
17	
18	| The user says | Do |
19	|---|---|
20	| An unambiguous yes ("go", "build it") | Step 2 |
21	| A yes with a change (another cap, a task held back) | Make it, say so, step 2 |
22	| A hedge, or anything else | Not a go. Answer, ask again |
23	
24	No live user: there is no go. Report what is ready and stop.
25	
26	**2. Start.** `dt build start M<n>`. It refuses on a dirty tree; otherwise it creates and
27	switches to `build/M<n>`, the branch every worktree starts from and every landing commits to, and
28	commits the go there as `**Build:**` in `tasks/plan.md`. A refusal is reported as it stands; fix nothing yourself.
29	
30	**3. Interface first.** The first `dt ready` lists only the interface task(s). After they land,
31	the interface review runs as a barrier: `dt ready` releases nothing else until it is recorded as
32	passed. A Critical there is a stop like any other.
33	
34	**4. Loop** (`loop.md`) until it ends: Exit when every task has landed and the exit review is
35	recorded, otherwise a stop reported per `escalation.md`.
36

1	# The dispatch loop
2	
3	Read with Run, Resume and Exit. Stops go to `escalation.md`.
4	
5	Work is dataflow, not waves: a task half starts the moment what it needs has landed, and `dt`
6	decides what that is. The driver only spawns what `dt ready` lists and lands what finishes.
7	
8	## Dispatch
9	
10	```
11	python3 ../../scripts/dt.py ready --reserve --cap 6 --json
12	```
13	
14	It prints one object: `rows` to spawn, `held` (halves waiting, each with its `why`), `holds`
15	(why nothing is released: a Critical or an escalation, `escalation.md`) and `all_landed`. Each row
16	is `{id, role, agent_type, title, kind, pkg, remaining_cp, prompt}`, already reserved and ordered
17	by remaining critical path. The cap counts reservations and live claims, so asking again
18	after every completion fills only the free slots. The user may set another cap; keep it for the run.
19	
20	Spawn every row in **one message**, one Agent call each:
21	
22	| Agent field | Value |
23	|---|---|
24	| `subagent_type` | the row's `agent_type` (`tester`, `implementer`, `reviewer`) |
25	| `isolation` | `worktree` |
26	| `run_in_background` | `true` |
27	| `description` | `<id> <role>` |
28	| `prompt` | the row's `prompt`, verbatim: one line naming the task, the role and the `dt` path |
29	
30	Rows with `agent_type` `reviewer` are checkpoint reviews: `dt` lists them once a checkpoint's
31	tasks have all landed (each package with changes, one marked lead). Spawn them the same way; their
32	worktrees keep their mutations out of the main checkout.
33	
34	Before spawning, nothing else: no reading the task, the packet or the plan. The agent runs
35	`dt claim`, which writes its packet, its edit list and its base in its own worktree.
36	
37	## Landing
38	
39	Each completion arrives on its own, as the agent's last message. Act on its status line
40	(`../SKILL.md`, Status lines), one landing at a time, in completion order:
41	
42	| Line | Run | Then |
43	|---|---|---|
44	| `DONE <task> <role>` or `STOPPED <task> <role>: …` | `dt land <task> --role <role>` | branch on the JSON below |
45	| `REVIEW <checkpoint> <pkg>: …` | `dt finding --from <reviews>/<checkpoint>.<pkg>.md --checkpoint <checkpoint> --pkg <pkg>`, where `<reviews>` is `$(git rev-parse --git-common-dir)/dt-build/reviews` | a Critical count above 0 → `escalation.md`, Critical. No findings file (the reviewer couldn't write it) → `dt build release <checkpoint>.<pkg> reviewer` and dispatch again; a second time is a stop (`escalation.md`, harness) |
46	| anything else | `dt land <task> --role <role> --harness` | the half goes back to `dt ready` |
47	
48	`dt land` prints `{landed, reject, kind, detail_file}`:
49	
50	| Result | Do |
51	|---|---|
52	| `landed: true` | dispatch again |
53	| `reject` `gate`, `writes`, `conflict`, `integration` | dispatch again: a first rejection comes back as a ready half whose claim prints it to the new agent; a second one blocks the half (`escalation.md`) |
54	| `reject` `escalated` | `escalation.md`, by `kind` |
55	| `reject` `harness` | dispatch again; the third in a row on one half blocks it |
56	
57	Never re-read the agent's message for more, never fix anything yourself, never retry a land by hand.
58	
59	## Draining and stopping
60	
61	A stop never interrupts an agent mid-task. While a stop is pending, keep landing the agents still
62	running and keep dispatching whatever `dt ready` still lists (it holds back what the stop
63	affects). When nothing is ready and nothing is running:
64	- **every task landed** and the exit review recorded → Exit (`exit.md`);
65	- otherwise, report the stop (`escalation.md`) and end the turn.
66	
67	After a compaction in the same session, run `dt build status`: a claim not yet finished belongs
68	to a running agent; leave it and wait for its completion. A finished claim nobody landed gets
69	`dt land` now.
70	
71	## The task list
72	
73	Keep one item per running half (`<id> <role>`), ticked when it lands or blocks, so the user sees
74	the build move without reading the transcript.

2

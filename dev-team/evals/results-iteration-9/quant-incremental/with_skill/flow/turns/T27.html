## Changed

- **QR-02:** now also requires the comparison page to load in ≤ 2 s and the spectrum page in ≤ 1 s.
- **QR-07:** the visual-design step now also covers the `RVIEW` pages.
- **QR-09** (new): a full study run finishes in ≤ 60 minutes.
- **Scope:** the "signal research, backtesting" exclusion now reads "return prediction, signal research, return-seeking backtests and paper trading". Costs, shorting and leverage, and learned estimators are now excluded too.
- **Summary, Users and Assumptions** are updated, and there is one new Changelog row.

## Choices of mine to check

These weren't in what you confirmed:

1. **Failed optimizations (PORT edge case):** if an optimization fails on a date, that portfolio is marked failed for that month, the date is listed in the report, and the month is left out of every comparison involving that portfolio. Each comparison then uses only the months both portfolios have.
2. **Crisis windows for the stability analysis (EVAL-04):** 2007-10 to 2009-03 and 2020-02 to 2020-04.
3. **Last rebalance date (STRUCT-01):** it's the last month-end whose following holding month is complete. With data ending 2026-10-16, that's the end of August 2026.
4. **Tolerances I set myself:**
   - Equal risk contribution: each name's risk share is within 1% of the average (PORT-02).
   - Known answers on the diagonal test data: equal risk contribution must match inverse-volatility weights, and HRP must match inverse-variance weights, each within 1e-6 (SYNTH-05).
5. **Gaps inside a holding month:** a held name with a missing day keeps its value unchanged that day, and the gap is listed in the report.

There are no open questions left; all four of yours are answered in the requirements.

Goals

goalG1Trust the data: no dead companies silently dropped, and no stock universe that peeks at the future.
served byBUILD, EDA, PRICE, UNIV
goalG2See every data problem: each check, each disagreement with a second source, and each fix is reported, never hidden.
served byBUILD, EDA, EXPL, HLTH
goalG3Reproduce any past result exactly, even after the data vendor revises its history.
served bySNAP
goalG4Load any slice of the data for research in a single step.
served byLOAD, PRICE
goalG5Explore the data by eye in a local app built for this work, not a generic dashboard.
served byEXPL

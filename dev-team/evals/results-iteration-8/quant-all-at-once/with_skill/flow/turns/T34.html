## Risks
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| LightGBM fits per feature × fold × horizon are slow on 15 years × 500 names | A full catalogue run takes hours | Single-threaded fits run in parallel across features; smoke uses 2 features on synthetic data; profile in M6-T12 |
| The information-gain test has low power at daily cross-sections | Real features fail even when weakly useful | TC-51 and TC-52 confirm the procedure finds a planted 0.1 correlation; failures are recorded, not hidden (SIG-04) |
| A feature definition leaks the future through a library helper | False discoveries | M6-T04's TC-48 look-ahead check runs on every library feature |

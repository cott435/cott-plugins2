# S&P 500 research data — References

### RF-01: PCA market-mode removal
**File:** `references/market_decomposition.py` · **Hash:** b3993e95 · **Kind:** code · **Milestones:** —
- **Purpose:** removes the top-k principal components (the market mode) from a daily returns panel to leave idiosyncratic residuals, and measures the cross-correlation left
- **Key parts:** `demean_returns(returns, min_obs)`, `standardise(returns)`, `pca_factors(returns, k)`, `residualize(returns, factors)`, `remove_market(returns, k)`, `residual_correlation_check(residuals)`
- **In → Out:** T×N DataFrame of simple daily returns (dates × tickers) → residuals T×N, factors T×k, loadings and betas N×k, explained variance (k), mean |off-diagonal corr| (float)
- **Use for:** the factor-removal step and its check that residual |corr| falls well below raw; a starting point for the eigen-decomposition that denoising builds on
- **Caveats:** means, standardisation, eigenvectors and betas are fit on the full panel (look-ahead; fit per training window); columns under `min_obs` are dropped silently; gaps (including non-member days) are filled with 0 before demeaning, biasing means, variances and correlations; k is hard-coded, with no Marchenko–Pastur cutoff; the sign fix `np.sign(sum)` zeroes a vector whose loadings sum to 0; zero-variance columns give NaNs that `nanmean` hides; betas are on the raw scale while factors are on the correlation scale

# US Equity Research Platform — Spec

**Status:** Draft · **Last updated:** 2026-10-18 · **Brief:** [brief.md](brief.md)

## Summary
A daily US equity dataset of S&P 500 members since 1998 with an as-of loader. Given any past date, the loader returns the stocks that were members on that date and their total returns, using only what was known then. Every download, clean, validate and store step can be run on demand. A validation layer checks the data against an independent source and flags every disagreement, and a browser app shows prices, corporate actions, source differences, universe size and live run progress. See the [brief](brief.md) for why.

## Users
| User | What they need to do |
|---|---|
| Researcher (the owner, sole user) | Refresh the data, ask for the universe and total returns as of any date since 1998, run validation, review and resolve flagged differences, inspect any stock visually, watch runs progress |

## Scope
**In scope:** Universe as of a date, Total returns as of a date, Data refresh, Cross-check and findings, Exploratory analysis, Data-quality app, Live run progress.

**Out of scope:**
- Company fundamentals such as earnings and balance sheets (a later stage).
- ETFs, over-the-counter stocks, preferred shares, and stocks that were never S&P 500 members.
- Intraday data and live market feeds.
- A second full copy of the data from the cross-check source: it spot-checks, it doesn't replace.
- Screeners or research views in the app: in this stage it is a data-quality tool.
- Signals, backtests and models.
- Scheduled or automatic refreshes: refreshes are started by the user.

## Features

### Universe as of a date (`UNIV`)
**Serves:** Goal 1
**Stage:** S1
**Behavior:** The researcher gives a date from 1998-01-02 onward and gets the list of stocks that were S&P 500 members on that date, including companies that later left the index, were acquired or went bankrupt. Each stock is identified by the company, not by its current ticker, and carries the ticker it traded under on that date.

**Requirements**
- **UNIV-01** For a given date, the system returns every stock that was an S&P 500 member during that date, by the date each index change took effect.
  - Given an index addition announced on day A and effective on day E > A, when the universe is requested for any day from A to E − 1, then the stock is not in it; when requested for day E, then it is.
  - Given an index removal effective on day R, when the universe is requested for day R − 1, then the stock is in it; for day R, then it is not.
- **UNIV-02** The universe for a date includes members that later left the index or stopped trading.
  - Given 2008-06-02, when the universe is requested, then Lehman Brothers, Bear Stearns, Countrywide, Merrill Lynch, Wachovia and Anheuser-Busch are all in it.
- **UNIV-03** Each stock has a permanent identifier for the company, which is unchanged across ticker changes.
  - Given a company that changed ticker, when its membership is requested for dates before and after the change, then both carry the same identifier.
- **UNIV-04** Each returned member carries the ticker it traded under on the requested date.
  - Given 2005-06-01, when the universe is requested, then the entry for Anheuser-Busch shows ticker BUD and the company is Anheuser-Busch, not today's holder of that ticker.
- **UNIV-05** The system returns the number of members for every trading day in a date range.
  - Given 1998-01-02 to the latest loaded day, when universe sizes are requested, then every trading day has a count, and each count equals the length of that day's universe list.

**Edge cases & errors**
- Date before 1998-01-02 → an error that names the earliest supported date; no partial list.
- Date after the latest loaded trading day → an error that names the latest loaded day.
- Weekend or market holiday → the universe for the previous trading day, with that day stated in the result.
- A day's member count outside 490–510 → the count is still returned, and a finding is raised (CHECK).

### Total returns as of a date (`RET`)
**Serves:** Goal 1
**Stage:** S1
**Behavior:** The researcher gives an as-of date and a set of stocks (usually that date's universe) and gets daily total-return series, including dividends and adjusted for splits, through the as-of date. Nothing published after the as-of date affects the result.

**Requirements**
- **RET-01** For every stock that was investable on any date since 1998, the system returns its daily total-return series between two given dates.
  - Given any date D since 1998-01-02 and the universe for D, when total returns from D to D + 20 trading days are requested, then a series is returned for every member, including members that stopped trading in that window.
- **RET-02** A daily total return combines the price change and any cash dividend going ex on that day.
  - Given a stock with a $0.50 dividend going ex on day T, a close of $50.00 on T − 1 and a close of $49.75 on T, when returns are requested, then the return for T is (49.75 + 0.50) / 50.00 − 1 = +0.50%.
- **RET-03** Daily total returns are unaffected by stock splits.
  - Given a 2-for-1 split effective on day T with closes of $100.00 on T − 1 and $50.50 on T, when returns are requested, then the return for T is +1.00%.
- **RET-04** Results requested as of date D use no information published after D.
  - Given a split or dividend that goes ex after D, when returns or prices are requested as of D, then the values up to D are identical to those produced before that event was loaded.
- **RET-05** Price levels are available unadjusted, or adjusted only for splits and dividends that went ex on or before the as-of date.
  - Given a 2-for-1 split on day S > D, when adjusted prices are requested as of D, then they equal the unadjusted prices for every day up to D.
- **RET-06** A stock's return series runs through its last trading day, including the return on that last day.
  - Given Lehman Brothers, when returns through 2008-09-30 are requested, then the return for 2008-09-15 is present and below −90%.
  - Given a company acquired for cash, when returns are requested, then the return on its last trading day is present and the series ends that day.
- **RET-07** Every returned series states whether each day is a trading day with a price, or a gap.
  - Given a trading halt with no close on day T, when returns are requested, then day T is marked as a gap and has no invented return.

**Edge cases & errors**
- Stock not a member on the as-of date → its series is still returned if asked for by identifier, with a note that it was not a member then.
- Requested range starts before a stock's first trading day or ends after its last → the series covers only the trading days, and the result says where it starts and ends.
- A day's return below −95% or above +100% → the value is returned unchanged, and a finding is raised (CHECK) unless one is already explained.

### Data refresh (`SYNC`)
**Serves:** Goal 1
**Stage:** S1
**Behavior:** The researcher starts a refresh. The system downloads what is new from the primary source (the existing Sharadar subscription), cleans it, validates it and stores it, so the data runs through the last completed trading day. The first refresh builds the full history from 1998.

**Requirements**
- **SYNC-01** A refresh downloads data from the primary source.
  - Given an empty store, when a refresh is started, then afterwards prices, corporate actions, company identities and index membership are stored for every S&P 500 member from 1998-01-02 to the last completed trading day.
- **SYNC-02** A refresh fetches only data newer than what is already stored.
  - Given data stored through day T, when a refresh is started on day T + 1 after the close, then only day T + 1 and any corrections the source issued since the last refresh are downloaded.
- **SYNC-03** A refresh cleans the data before it is stored.
  - Given a downloaded row with a duplicate (stock, date), when the refresh runs, then one row is stored and the duplicate is reported in the run summary.
- **SYNC-04** A refresh ends with a validation run (CHECK-01), and its results go to the run summary.
  - Given a completed refresh, when its summary is viewed, then it lists the number of rows added, rows changed, and new findings.
- **SYNC-05** A refresh that fails leaves the stored data as it was before the refresh started.
  - Given a refresh interrupted after half its downloads, when the universe or returns are requested, then the results are identical to those before the refresh.
- **SYNC-06** A refresh started while another is running is refused.
  - Given a refresh in progress, when a second is started, then it is refused with a message naming the running one.
- **SYNC-07** Each run of a refresh or validation is started on demand, from the command line or from the app.
  - Given the app open, when the researcher starts a refresh there, then it runs exactly as one started from the command line.

**Edge cases & errors**
- Primary source unreachable or credentials rejected → the refresh stops, stores nothing, and states the reason.
- The source revises a past value → the revision is stored, the earlier value is kept with the date it was replaced, and the run summary lists it.
- No new trading day since the last refresh → the refresh completes with zero rows added.

### Cross-check and findings (`CHECK`)
**Serves:** Goal 2
**Stage:** S1
**Behavior:** A validation run compares the data with an independent source (Yahoo Finance) for a sample of members, and runs internal checks on stocks that source doesn't carry. Every disagreement becomes a finding. The researcher marks each finding explained (with a reason) or accepted; the data itself is never edited to hide a finding.

**Requirements**
- **CHECK-01** A validation run checks 100 members that the independent source still carries, sampled across 1998 to today.
  - Given a validation run, when it finishes, then it has compared exactly 100 stocks (or all available, if fewer), and every calendar year from 1998 to today has at least 2 stocks that were members in that year.
- **CHECK-02** For each sampled stock, the run also checks every split and special dividend that stock had.
  - Given a sampled stock with 3 splits and 1 special dividend since 1998, when the run finishes, then all 4 events have been compared.
- **CHECK-03** A full validation run, on demand, checks every member the independent source carries.
  - Given a full validation run, when it finishes, then every member the independent source carries has been compared.
- **CHECK-04** Splits agree only when the date and the ratio match exactly.
  - Given a 3-for-2 split on one source and a 2-for-1 split on the same date on the other, when compared, then a finding is raised.
- **CHECK-05** Dividends agree only when the ex-date matches and the amount is within $0.001.
  - Given amounts of $0.2500 and $0.2509 on the same ex-date, when compared, then they agree; given $0.2500 and $0.2515, then a finding is raised.
- **CHECK-06** Daily total returns agree when they are within 5 basis points (0.05 percentage points) of each other.
  - Given returns of +1.20% and +1.24% for the same stock and day, when compared, then they agree; given +1.20% and +1.26%, then a finding is raised.
- **CHECK-07** Unadjusted closes agree when they are within 0.5% of each other.
  - Given closes of $40.00 and $40.18, when compared, then they agree; given $40.00 and $40.25, then a finding is raised.
- **CHECK-08** A stock the independent source doesn't carry is checked for missing trading days before its last trading day.
  - Given a delisted stock with no price on a day when the exchange was open and the stock was not halted, when checked, then a finding is raised for that day.
- **CHECK-09** A stock acquired for a known cash price is checked for a last close consistent with the deal.
  - Given a cash acquisition at $70.00 a share and a last close of $69.95, when checked, then it passes; given a last close more than 2% from the deal price, then a finding is raised.
- **CHECK-10** A stock's index membership is checked to end no later than its last trading day.
  - Given a stock whose membership ends after its last trading day, when checked, then a finding is raised.
- **CHECK-11** Findings from internal checks are listed and resolved in the same way as disagreements with the independent source.
  - Given a CHECK-08 finding and a CHECK-06 finding, when findings are listed, then both appear in one list with the same fields and the same resolve actions.
- **CHECK-12** Each finding records the stock, the date(s), the check that raised it, both values, and the difference.
  - Given any finding, when viewed, then all five are shown.
- **CHECK-13** The researcher resolves a finding by marking it explained, with a written reason, or accepted.
  - Given an open finding, when marked explained with no reason, then it is refused; with a reason, then its status becomes explained and the reason and date are kept.
- **CHECK-14** A resolved finding stays resolved in later runs while both values are unchanged.
  - Given an explained finding, when the next run finds the same difference, then no new finding is raised; when either value has changed, then the finding is reopened.
- **CHECK-15** The 10 named former members are present with both end dates matching the reference table in DATA-04.
  - Given each of the 10 names, when checked, then its index exit date and its last trading day on a major exchange each equal the table's value, or a finding is raised for each one that differs.

**Edge cases & errors**
- Independent source unreachable or rate-limited → the run retries up to 3 times per stock, then records the stock as not checked; it never records a pass.
- The independent source has no data for a sampled stock on a day → recorded as not compared, not as a disagreement.
- The independent source adjusts dividends differently from the primary source → returns are compared from each source's own unadjusted prices and dividends, so the difference in method raises no finding by itself.

### Exploratory analysis (`EDA`)
**Serves:** Goal 2
**Stage:** S1
**Behavior:** The researcher runs a basic exploratory analysis of the stored data and gets a report describing coverage, distributions and suspicious values. Anything suspicious is raised as a finding.

**Requirements**
- **EDA-01** The exploratory analysis is run with the `data-analysis` skill on the full stored history. It covers summary statistics and tails of daily total returns by year, missing days per stock, universe size over time, and the number of corporate actions per year.
  - Given a completed refresh, when the analysis runs, then its report has a section for each of the four, covering every year from 1998 to today.
- **EDA-02** Each anomaly the analysis identifies becomes a finding.
  - Given a stock with a daily return above +100% and no corporate action on that day, when the analysis runs, then a finding is raised for that stock and day.
- **EDA-03** The analysis report can be opened in the app.
  - Given a completed analysis, when the researcher opens it in the app, then the report's tables and charts are shown.

**Edge cases & errors**
- Analysis started with no stored data → refused with a message to run a refresh first.

### Data-quality app (`VIEW`)
**Serves:** Goal 3
**Stage:** S1
**Behavior:** A browser app where the researcher picks a stock or a date and sees its data and quality issues without writing code: prices and adjustments, a corporate-action timeline, differences against the independent source, universe size over time, and the list of findings. Charts are built with the `dataviz` skill; the visual design follows the `frontend-design` skill.

**Requirements**
- **VIEW-01** For a chosen stock, the app shows its unadjusted and adjusted price history.
  - Given Eastman Kodak, when chosen, then both price series are shown for 1998 to its last trading day.
- **VIEW-02** For a chosen stock, the app shows a timeline of its corporate actions, ticker changes and index entry and exit.
  - Given Wachovia, when chosen, then its index exit and last trading day appear on the timeline.
- **VIEW-03** For a chosen stock that was compared, the app shows its daily differences from the independent source over time, with the agreement thresholds marked.
  - Given a sampled stock, when chosen, then each day beyond a CHECK-06 or CHECK-07 threshold is highlighted.
- **VIEW-04** The app shows S&P 500 member count per trading day from 1998 to the latest loaded day.
  - Given the chart, when the researcher points at a day, then that day's count and date are shown.
- **VIEW-05** The app lists findings, filterable by status, check and stock.
  - Given 50 findings of which 10 are open, when filtered to open, then exactly 10 are shown.
- **VIEW-06** The researcher can resolve a finding from the app.
  - Given an open finding, when marked explained with a reason in the app, then its status changes as in CHECK-13.
- **VIEW-07** For a chosen date, the app shows that date's universe.
  - Given 2008-06-02, when chosen, then the list shows every member with its ticker on that date.
- **VIEW-08** The app shows only data views and quality tools; it has no screening or research views.
  - Given the app, when every page is opened, then none ranks or filters stocks by return or signal.

**Edge cases & errors**
- A stock with no comparison data → the differences view says it was not sampled and offers a full check.
- Stored data being refreshed while the app is open → the app keeps showing the previous data until the refresh completes, then says new data is available.

### Live run progress (`RUNS`)
**Serves:** Goal 3
**Stage:** S1
**Behavior:** While a refresh or validation run is going, the app shows its progress live: the current step, how far along it is, and errors as they happen. Finished runs stay listed with their summaries.

**Requirements**
- **RUNS-01** The app shows a running refresh's or validation's current step and percentage complete, updated as the run advances.
  - Given a running refresh, when the app is open, then the shown step and percentage change within 2 seconds of the run advancing, without reloading the page.
- **RUNS-02** Errors and warnings appear in the app as they happen during a run.
  - Given a stock the independent source rejects, when it happens, then a warning naming the stock appears within 2 seconds.
- **RUNS-03** A run started from the command line also shows its progress in the app.
  - Given a refresh started from the command line, when the app is opened, then the run is shown with its current progress.
- **RUNS-04** Finished runs are listed with start time, end time, outcome and summary.
  - Given 3 finished runs, when the run list is opened, then all 3 are shown, newest first.

**Edge cases & errors**
- The app is opened mid-run → it shows the current state immediately, not only the next update.
- The app loses its connection mid-run → it says so, reconnects, and catches up without restarting the run.

## Data requirements
- **DATA-01** Daily data from 1998-01-02 to the last completed trading day: unadjusted open, high, low, close and volume for every stock that was an S&P 500 member on any day in that range, for every day it traded on a major US exchange.
  - Given any member on any date since 1998-01-02, when its prices are requested, then a close exists for every trading day it was listed on a major exchange.
- **DATA-02** Every split, cash dividend (regular and special), ticker change, merger, acquisition and delisting for those stocks since 1998, with its effective or ex-date.
  - Given any of the 10 names in DATA-04, when its corporate actions are requested, then its final event (acquisition, merger or delisting) is present.
- **DATA-03** S&P 500 membership history since 1998 with the effective date of every addition and removal, including companies that later left or were delisted; no ETFs, over-the-counter stocks or preferred shares.
  - Given the stored membership, when every member on every date is listed, then none is an ETF, an over-the-counter listing or a preferred share.
- **DATA-04** The 10 named former members are present with both an index exit date and a last trading day on a major exchange, each equal to this reference table:

  | Ticker then | Company | Index exit (first day not a member) | Last major-exchange trading day | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | BUD | Anheuser-Busch | 2008-11-19 | 2008-11-18 | Acquired by InBev for cash |
  | DNA | Genentech | 2009-03-27 | 2009-03-26 | Acquired by Roche; whether it was ever an S&P 500 member is uncertain (Roche held a majority stake) |
  | WB | Wachovia | 2009-01-02 | 2008-12-31 | Acquired by Wells Fargo for stock |
  | LEH | Lehman Brothers | 2008-09-17 | 2008-09-17 | Bankruptcy filed 2008-09-15; then traded over the counter |
  | BSC | Bear Stearns | 2008-06-02 | 2008-05-30 | Acquired by JPMorgan Chase for stock |
  | CFC | Countrywide Financial | 2008-07-02 | 2008-07-01 | Acquired by Bank of America for stock |
  | MER | Merrill Lynch | 2009-01-02 | 2008-12-31 | Acquired by Bank of America for stock |
  | SGP | Schering-Plough | 2009-11-04 | 2009-11-03 | Merged with Merck; the Schering-Plough legal entity was renamed Merck & Co. |
  | WYE | Wyeth | 2009-10-16 | 2009-10-15 | Acquired by Pfizer for cash and stock |
  | EK | Eastman Kodak | 2010-12-20 | 2012-01-19 | Left the index 2010; bankruptcy 2012, then traded over the counter |

  - Given each row, when its stored index exit and last trading day are compared with the table, then both match.
- **DATA-05** Every stored value carries the date it was first known to the system, and every replaced value is kept with the date it was replaced.
  - Given a value revised by the source on day V, when data is requested as known on V − 1, then the earlier value is returned.

## Quality requirements
- **QR-01** The universe and total returns for one date come back in ≤ 2 seconds.
  - Timed on the researcher's machine for 20 random dates since 1998; every one ≤ 2 s.
- **QR-02** Full daily total-return series from 1998-01-02 to the latest loaded day for one date's ~500 members come back in ≤ 30 seconds.
  - Timed on the researcher's machine for 5 random dates; every one ≤ 30 s.
- **QR-03** No data is bought beyond the existing primary-source subscription, and the independent source is used only within its free access.
  - Checked at architecture review: every data source used is the existing subscription or free.
- **QR-04** The independent source is used only for spot-checks: no more than the CHECK-01 sample is fetched per validation run unless a full check is requested.
  - Given a standard validation run, when its fetch log is read, then at most 100 stocks were requested.
- **QR-05** Data-source credentials are never shown in the app, in run logs or in run summaries.
  - Given a refresh with credentials configured, when its log, summary and the app are searched for the credential value, then it appears nowhere.

## Assumptions
- One user, on one machine; no sign-in or sharing.
- The primary source's history includes delisted companies and correct index entry and exit dates back to 1998.
- The independent source lacks most delisted names and adjusts dividends its own way, so it is compared only on unadjusted prices, raw dividends and splits.
- Refreshes are started by the user, not on a schedule.

## Open questions
- **The DATA-04 dates are drafts. The researcher verifies each of the 20 dates** before they are used as expected values. The least certain are LEH and EK (the last major-exchange trading day versus the move to over-the-counter trading), DNA (whether Genentech was ever a member), and every index exit date (the day S&P's change took effect). (Answered by the researcher.)
- **Identity after SGP's merger:** the Schering-Plough legal entity continued as Merck & Co. under a new ticker. The spec treats Schering-Plough as ending on 2009-11-03, but the source may carry it as one continuous company. (Answered by a probe during architecture.)
- **Does the primary source carry S&P 500 membership with effective dates back to 1998, including the 10 names?** (Answered by a probe during architecture.)

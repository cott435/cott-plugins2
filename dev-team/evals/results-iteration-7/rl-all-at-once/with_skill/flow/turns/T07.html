| 2 | **Train reference** | `rllab reference <config> --seeds 0-4` | Trains the Stable-Baselines3 learner on the same world and budget, into the same run-folder format | S1 (also S2, S3 Phase A) |

| 4 | **Watch** | `rllab view <policy> [<policy2>] --seed N` | Records an episode from each checkpoint or baseline and plays one, or two side by side | S1 |
| 5 | **Lesson note** | `rllab note <world>` / `rllab status <world>` | Draws the learning-curve and diagnostic charts, refreshes the note's generated blocks, and reports whether the world is done | S1 |
| 6 | **Cross-play** | `rllab crossplay <phase-b-run>` | Plays every prey snapshot against every predator snapshot and writes the 11×11 table, the heatmap, and the comparison table for PREY-15 | S3 |

Goals

served byEVAL, FEAT, FTEST, ING, LOAD, PAPER, UNIV, VAL

requirementCOV-01The system produces a sample covariance estimate per window.
criteria
  • Given a window with T days and N stocks, when the estimate is made, then it is symmetric and matches a direct calculation within 1e-10.
covered byPL-03
requirementCOV-02The system produces a shrinkage estimate that pulls the sample covariance toward a structured target, with the shrinkage amount estimated from the data.
criteria
  • Given a window, when the shrinkage estimate is made, then the shrinkage amount (between 0 and 1) is recorded, and the estimate is positive definite.
covered byPL-03

criteria
  • Given a window, when the denoised estimate is made, then eigenvalues above the edge are unchanged, the trace of the correlation matrix equals N within 1e-8, and the estimate is positive definite.
  • Given simulated pure-noise returns, when denoised, then the denoised correlation matrix is closer to the identity (smaller Frobenius distance) than the sample one.
covered byPL-03
requirementCOV-04The estimation window length defaults to 756 trading days, and 504 and 1260 can also be chosen.
criteria
  • Given a window length setting, when estimates are made, then each window uses exactly that many trading days.
covered byPL-03
requirementCOV-05The estimators are compared on real data with the data-analysis skill. It is given each estimator's out-of-sample minimum-variance portfolio variance per rebalance, and its out-of-sample covariance forecast error per window, over the development period.
criteria
  • Given a completed S2 walk-forward run, when the comparison runs, then the report shows each estimator's realized variance and forecast error, with the spread over time and its stability across sub-periods (2005–2009, 2010–2014, 2015–2019, 2020 to the holdout start).
covered byPL-03
requirementCOV-06Any claim that one estimator beats another is tested with the null-hypothesis-testing skill. It is given the paired per-rebalance realized-variance differences over the development period. The claim passes only if the null of no difference is rejected at 5%, after correction for all estimator pairs compared.
criteria
  • Given the claim "denoised beats sample", when tested, then the report states the test, the p-value, the corrected verdict and the effect size.

requirementFPORT-03A forecast portfolio "beats the classical portfolios" only if all of these hold, each tested with the null-hypothesis-testing skill at 5% after correction across all comparisons:
criteria

  • its realized variance is lower than that of the risk parity, hierarchical risk parity and equal-weight portfolios (null of equal variance rejected);
  • against each minimum-variance portfolio, its realized variance is not significantly higher (null of equal variance not rejected in the "higher" direction).

criteria

  • Given a holdout fail and a paper-trading pass, when the verdict is recorded, then it is "does not beat".

requirementPAPER-05A paper test counts toward a verdict only after at least 63 trading days (about 3 months). The pass is directional, with no significance test deciding it. It passes only if, over the same days, the candidate's after-cost Sharpe ratio is higher than every S2 portfolio's, and its realized variance is lower than that of risk parity, HRP and equal weight. The minimum-variance comparison has no directional form, so it is decided only by the walk-forward and holdout tests (FPORT-03). The null-hypothesis-testing skill is also run on the same comparisons. Its p-values are reported for information only and do not change the pass or fail.
criteria
  • Given 63 or more days where the candidate meets every directional condition, when the p-values are not significant, then the paper test still passes.
  • Given 40 trading days of paper results, when a verdict is requested, then the answer is "not enough days yet".
covered byPL-08

criteria

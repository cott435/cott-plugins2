*Written by `dt roadmap` from doc statuses and todo checkboxes; never hand-edited.

## S1: Trusted data & explorer
**Outline:** Download, clean, validate and store daily S&P 500 data with membership on each date, delisted stocks and corporate actions, explore it, and watch download and validation runs live in a local browser app.
**Skills:** dev-team:api-probe, data-analysis, dataviz, frontend-design

## S2: Risk structure & classical portfolios
**Outline:** Remove the market and other dominant factors, denoise the correlation matrix, and rank covariance estimators and minimum-variance, risk-parity and hierarchical risk-parity portfolios on realised out-of-sample variance, using the shared walk-forward engine with every comparison recorded in the hypothesis ledger.
**Skills:** data-analysis, null-hypothesis-testing

## S3: Features & significance
**Outline:** Build candidate features and test each for information gain over market-removed returns, with every claim tested against chance and its outcome recorded in the ledger.
**Skills:** null-hypothesis-testing, data-analysis

## S4: Forecasts & agents
**Outline:** Forecast returns and volatility with neural networks on the surviving features against simple baselines, then run forecast-driven portfolios and PPO agents in simulated backtests that must beat the stage-2 portfolios out of sample after costs, or stop.
**Skills:** null-hypothesis-testing, data-analysis

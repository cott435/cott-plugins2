## What changed
**Two new decisions in `docs/architecture.md`:**
- **AD-19: lockbox scoring for forecast portfolios and agents.** `models` scores its own entries. The scorer refits the method from its frozen registration on the training window just before the lockbox, then backtests the lockbox once. `ForecastRun` keeps covering the walk-forward periods only. The alternatives either touched lockbox data before the walk-forward result was recorded, or put `models` code into `lab`, which the dependency rules forbid.
- **AD-20: the `qrp` launcher.** Each package's `__main__.py` owns its own subcommands. A stdlib-only `qrp.py` at the workspace root maps `qrp <pkg> …` to `python -m qrp_<pkg>` in a subprocess.

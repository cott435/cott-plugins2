The S4/S5 architecture is written as a Draft. Every S4 and S5 requirement has a home, GATE-05, GATE-06 and DATA-09's changes are absorbed, and `dt lint` has 0 errors.

## What changed
**Three new packages,** each depending only downward:

| Package | Modules | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| `trading` (no neural code) | `env`, `baselines`, `scoring`, `benchmark`, `paper`, `reports`, `cli` | The environment with 10 bp costs from drifted weights, S2's 12 strategies and equal weight as competitors, Sharpe after costs and deflated Sharpe, benchmark naming, paper recording (deadline enforced from the UTC timestamp), settlement and verdicts |
| `forecast` (PyTorch) | `inputs`, `targets`, `classical`, `neural`, `claims`, `portfolios`, `paper`, `reports`, `cli` | Classical and neural forecasters, the two portfolio types, claims through the ledger, freeze, and its own monthly paper step |

**Root architecture:**
- **System map:** the overview, diagram, package table, dependency rules and layout now include the three packages.
- **New system pipelines:**
  - **PL-22: Forecast development.** Score S2 and name the benchmark, then forecasters, then portfolios scored by `trading`.
  - **PL-23: Monthly paper trading.** `forecast` and `agents` record weights through `trading`, and `trading` settles into the ledger.
  - **PL-24: Agent training and evaluation.**
- **Tech stack:** PyTorch 2.5, arch 7, and Stable-Baselines3 2.4 with Gymnasium 1.0 (reference only).
- **New risks:** PPO may not learn on the data available, and paper verdicts arrive about 3 years after freezing.

**New decisions (Proposed):**
- **AD-27:** the three packages, plus the key split. Producing paper weights lives in each competitor's package; settling and judging lives in `trading`. That keeps every dependency pointing down without a plug-in registry. The ledger stays in `features`.

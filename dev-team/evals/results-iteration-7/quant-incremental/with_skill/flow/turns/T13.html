## Assumptions
- **Check names:** `close_not_positive`, `high_below_low`, `close_outside_range`, `duplicate_conflict`, `stale_price`, `missing`, `large_move_no_action`, `member_count`, `known_actions`, `history_floor`, `cross_source`.
- **"Stale" rule:** a run of 5 equal closes flags all 5 days. The spec's "unchanged for 5 or more days" could also be read as needing 6 equal closes.
- **Split ratios:** stored as new shares per old share (4.0 for 4-for-1, 0.1 for 1-for-10).
- **Fingerprints:** v1 and v2 in TC-18 stand in for real 64-character fingerprints.
- **Real-data cases:** they record the snapshot hash or version fingerprint at the first real build, and are skipped until then.

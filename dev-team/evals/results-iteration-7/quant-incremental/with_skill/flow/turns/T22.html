## Summary
A local, trustworthy daily US stock dataset (prices, returns, corporate actions and a universe matching each past date, including delisted companies) built from dated download snapshots, validated against a second source, and served through a loader that never returns information from the future. A browser app lets the single user explore the data, inspect every validation result, and start and watch build runs live. See the [brief](brief.md) for why.

- Intraday data, options, futures and crypto.
- Live trading, real money and any brokerage connection.

## Quality requirements

## Numbers I added beyond your defaults (please check)
- **Skipped months:** a month with fewer than **20 eligible stocks** is skipped for every strategy.
- **Minimum to test:** a comparison needs at least **36 holding months**. With fewer, it's reported as "too few months to test".
- **Known-answer checks:**
  - pure noise gives 0 signal factors in at least 95% of windows over 50 seeds
  - a planted factor's residual correlation falls to a quarter or less of the raw correlation
  - the factor model's estimates land within 10% of the planted covariances
- **Statistical test check:** a planted 20% variance reduction over 200 months must be detected.
- **Failed estimator:** if an estimator fails in a month, its three strategies hold equal weight that month, and the month is flagged.

2	
3	Read when writing a milestone's tasks. Examples: `examples-phase0.md`.

10	## Phase 2: Wiring and thicken     wiring, one per pipeline      its entry module, smoke test

12	## Phase 3: System                 system (solo, last)           fakes swapped for real providers
13	```

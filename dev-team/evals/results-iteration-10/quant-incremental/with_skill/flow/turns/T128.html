# forecast — Architecture

**Package:** forecast · **Status:** Draft · **Last updated:** 2026-10-18 · **System:** [../../architecture.md](../../architecture.md)

## Overview
The forecast study, started with `eqdata-forecast` (AD-25). `inputs` loads the feature study's inputs through `eqdata_features.load_inputs`, cut at the fixed holdout boundary unless the holdout is being scored, and reads which candidates passed on the development months from the latest `features` report (AD-26). For each rebalance date, `samples` takes the window, spectrum and holding month from `eqdata_study`, the residuals and the 22 candidates from `eqdata_features`, and builds each eligible member's 252-day sequence, its passed features and both targets. `network` trains the sequence model and `forecasts` runs it and the four baselines walk-forward on the feature study's refit schedule (AD-27, AD-28). `classical` forms the portfolio study's 12 portfolios and 1/N through `eqdata_study`, and `rules` forms the three forecast-driven ones (AD-29). `backtest` holds every portfolio through its month, charges its costs and measures it (AD-29). `inference` tests the forecasts against their baselines and the portfolios against their comparators. `profile` profiles the forecasts and the backtest. `report` keeps the holdout record and saves the five sections, with the sleeves and forecast summaries the agent study reads (AD-30).

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  cli --> settings & inputs & samples & network & forecasts & classical & rules & backtest & inference & profile & report
  inputs --> features[(eqdata_features exported API)] & eqdata[(eqdata public API)]
  samples --> inputs & study[(eqdata_study exported API)] & features
  network --> samples & settings
  forecasts --> network & samples & settings & features
  classical --> study & backtest
  rules --> forecasts & study & backtest & settings
  backtest --> study
  inference --> forecasts & backtest & settings
  profile --> samples & forecasts & backtest
  report --> profile & inference & backtest & forecasts & settings & eqdata
```

**Skills:** `profile` runs the `data-analysis` skill for FCST-13 and FPORT-08. It takes every month's samples, forecasts and held months, and the skill decides the summaries and plots within the profile and backtest sections. `inference` runs the `null-hypothesis-testing` skill for FCST-11, FCST-12 and FPORT-06. It takes the paired monthly series and the families AD-28 and AD-29 fix, and the skill picks each test statistic and how its p-value is computed. The report's charts are drawn by `web`'s `charts`, which follows the `dataviz` skill.

## Interfaces
See [contract.md](contract.md). `forecast` consumes [eqdata's contract](../eqdata/contract.md) (public API only), the symbols `eqdata_study` exports ([study's contract](../study/contract.md)) and the symbols `eqdata_features` exports ([features' contract](../features/contract.md)). It provides `agents` the backtest's cost and measures, the sleeve tape and the comparison row, which its `__init__` exports, and nothing else (AD-25).

## Cross-cutting concerns
- **No look-ahead (BT-07, INFO-03):** only `inputs` reads the panels, and a development run's panels end on 2021-09-30. Everything formed on R reads only the window, its bars and the feature values on R. Only `samples` reads the month after R, for the targets, and only `backtest` for the held returns. A refit trains only on months whose target month ended by the refit (FCST-05). Tests change a price, volume or return after R, or after 2021-09-30, and check that every weight and forecast for R is identical.
- **Simulated only (BT-02):** no component imports a network client; import-linter forbids `requests`, `httpx` and `urllib` in `eqdata_forecast`, and a test runs the study with the network disabled.
- **Reproducibility:** one generator seeded from `settings.toml` for every shuffle, and the network's seed and deterministic kernels from the same seed. `known_on`, the phase, the settings, their digest and the `features` report used go into the report's params.
- **Speed (FCST-15, QR-06):** one panel and bar load, one spectrum per window, ~23 network refits, ~2,700 classical and ~1,000 forecast-rule solves. The network trains on the CPU by default; `device = "cuda"` runs the same command on a rented GPU. Timed by a benchmark test on the full history.
- **Data:** built and tested on synthetic stores with planted return and volatility signals. The real run waits for PB-01, as the other studies do.

## Overview
The agent study, started with `eqdata-agents` (AD-25). `tape` reads the sleeves and forecast summaries the latest `forecast` report saved, through `eqdata_forecast.load_tape`, cut at the fixed holdout boundary unless the holdout is being scored (AD-30). It never reruns a forecast and reads nothing else from the store. `market` turns a tape into a monthly allocation environment: observations, sleeve weights, stock-level costs through `eqdata_forecast.cost`, and the risk-adjusted reward (AD-31). `train` trains PPO agents on episodes of development months, one process per seed (AD-31). `select` chooses the settings on the last 36 development months, and `evaluate` runs the 10-seed average and the baselines through the same environment (AD-32). `synthetic` builds the planted-regime and no-regime tapes the controls train on. `inference` tests the averaged agent and the controls. `report` keeps the holdout record and saves the five sections and the trust notice (AD-30, AD-32).

```mermaid
  cli --> settings & tape & market & synthetic & train & select & evaluate & inference & report
  tape --> forecast[(eqdata_forecast exported API)] & eqdata[(eqdata public API)]
  market --> tape & forecast & settings
  synthetic --> tape & settings
  train --> market & settings
  select --> train & evaluate & settings
  evaluate --> market & train & forecast
  inference --> evaluate & train & synthetic & forecast & settings
  report --> evaluate & inference & train & settings & eqdata
```

## Cross-cutting concerns
- **No look-ahead (BT-07, INFO-03, AGENT-06):** only `tape` reads the forecast report, and a development tape ends before 2021-09-30. An observation for R reads only tape rows for R and earlier; the month after R reaches only the reward. Every training episode is made of development months, and the settings are chosen on agents that never saw the last 36 (AGENT-07). A test changes a tape value after R and checks that every allocation on R or earlier is identical.
- **Reproducibility:** every seed, the bootstrap and every shuffle come from one `SeedSequence` from `settings.toml`, a child per agent and per test. The `forecast` report used, the settings and their digest go into the report's params.
- **Speed (AGENT-17, QR-06):** CPU only. Each environment step is a few vector operations on precomputed sleeve paths. Agents train in `workers` processes: the selection grid, the 10 final seeds and 10 seeds per control. Timed by a benchmark test on the full history.
- **Data:** built and tested on synthetic tapes first; the real run needs a real forecast report.

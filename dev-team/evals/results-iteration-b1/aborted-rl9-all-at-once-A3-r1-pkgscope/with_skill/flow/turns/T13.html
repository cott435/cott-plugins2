1	# Resume
2	
3	Read when a build is recorded and this session has started no agents: a new session after a
4	stop, a crash or a closed terminal. No agent of the old session is still running, so every claim
5	is either finished work to land or a dead agent's to release. The loop is `loop.md`; stops are
6	`escalation.md`.
7	
8	Everything the driver needs is on disk: `dt-build/` under the git common directory, the
9	`Dt-Landed:` trailers on `build/M<n>` (never commit subjects, which an agent could imitate), the
10	`tasks/todo.md` ticks, the findings in `tasks/plan.md` and the recorded go. Nothing from an
11	earlier conversation counts, approvals least of all.
12	
13	## Steps
14	
15	**1. Read the state.** `dt build status`. The main checkout must be on `build/M<n>` and clean; if
16	not, report it and stop (never stash, reset or switch yourself).
17	
18	**2. Check the go.** It stands for this milestone and this plan version. If `go_current` in
19	`dt build status` is false, a Replan changed the tasks since: ask for a new go (what changed, the
20	halves left), record it with `dt build go --note "<their words>"`, and stop the turn until it comes.
21	
22	**3. Reconcile, one row at a time:**
23	
24	| `dt build status` shows | Do |
25	|---|---|
26	| a claim marked finished | `dt land <task> --role <role>`: the gate re-runs if no verdict matches, so finished work isn't lost. Branch on the result as `loop.md` says |
27	| a claim not finished, or a reservation | its agent died with the old session: `dt build release <task> <role>`; the half comes back through `dt ready` (tasks are XS or S, so redoing is cheap) |
28	| a tester half landed, its implementer not | nothing: the implementer half is ready |
29	| a review reserved but not recorded | `dt build release <checkpoint>.<pkg> reviewer`; `dt ready` lists the review again |
30	| a barrier unpassed, a Critical open, a half blocked | stays until the user decides (`escalation.md`); report it with the rest |
31	
32	**4. Report, then continue.** One message: what landed before, what was re-landed or released
33	now, what is blocked and the decision it needs. With a decision pending and nothing ready, end the
34	turn there. Otherwise run the loop (`loop.md`).
35

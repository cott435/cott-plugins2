The reference index is Approved, and RF-02 and RF-03 stay unassigned. The auto-commit had already picked up both reference files. The next step is refining the idea, and nothing gets written until you've answered the questions below.

**Restated:** this adds neural forecasting and learned trading agents on top of the S2 and S3 work. You'd find out whether return and volatility forecasts, and agents trained on them, beat the variance-first portfolios out of sample in research and paper trading.

**How might we** test whether learned forecasts and agents add value beyond classical portfolios, when the sample is small and noisy and has already been looked at once?

**What it touches:**
- **Goals:** it needs a new Goal 8. The brief would then have 8 goals, so I'll ask whether to merge some.
- **Spec out-of-scope lines it reverses:** "trading signals, strategies, backtest returns or Sharpe ratios", "the portfolios use no return predictions, features or machine learning", and "turnover limits and transaction costs".
- **Data it builds on:**
  - S3's surviving features (FREG/GAIN)
  - S2's portfolios and walk-forward engine (PORT, WALK, EVAL)
  - S2's residuals (STRUC-03)

**Honest pushback before we go further:**
1. **S3 may find nothing.** The likeliest S3 outcome is "no feature survives". The idea needs a defined path for that case, or a gate that waits on S3's result.
2. **The clean holdout is gone.** S3 opens 2022 onward once, to confirm features. Anything S4 selects with those features is already conditioned on that period. The only truly unseen data left is the future, so forward paper trading becomes the real test.
3. **There's little data for deep models.** About 20 years of monthly decisions on roughly 500 names is small for a temporal fusion transformer (TFT), and smaller still for PPO. PPO agents on daily equities are known to overfit backtests. Without transaction costs, they learn to trade noise.
4. **"Beat" needs a definition.** The S2 portfolios are judged on variance, while forecast portfolios and agents aim at returns. The comparison needs one shared metric, net of costs, with a significance test that accounts for every model tried.
5. **It's two ideas.** Forecasting plus forecast-driven portfolios is one outcome; PPO agents is another. Each could succeed or fail independently.

### Questions
1. **Should this be one stage or two?** My default is two: S4 "Forecasts and forecast portfolios" first, then S5 "PPO allocation agents", each with its own idea file.
2. **What does "beat" mean?** My default is net-of-cost Sharpe ratio against the best S2 portfolio, with a paired Sharpe-difference test and a deflated-Sharpe correction over all models tried. The forecasts themselves must also beat simple baselines on their own error measures (QLIKE for volatility, rank IC for returns).
3. **What is the final test?** My default:
   - Walk-forward development on 2007–2021.
   - 2022 onward reported, but labelled as already seen by S3.
   - A forward paper-trading period from the day the models are frozen, as the deciding test.
4. **Transaction costs?** My default is yes: 10 bp per side, with turnover reported. This reverses the S2 out-of-scope line for S4 and S5 only.
5. **What if no feature survives S3?** My default is that S4 still runs on price and volume history plus the three controls, and the report says so. S4 isn't blocked.

### Variations
1. **Baselines first:** historical mean or zero for returns, and EWMA and HAR models for volatility. Every neural model must beat these.
2. **Volatility first:** forecast volatility only, where forecasts are known to work, and feed it into the S2 portfolios. This stays true to the project's variance-first framing and is the most likely win.
3. **TFT return and volatility forecasts driving mean-variance portfolios:** expected returns are shrunk hard toward zero, with S2's 5% cap and long-only rules.
4. **PPO meta-allocator:** the agent chooses weights across a few portfolios (S2's portfolios plus the forecast portfolio) instead of 500 stocks. The action space is small enough to learn and to check.
5. **PPO per-stock agent on the top-100 universe:** this is the literal version of the idea, and the hardest to make honest.
6. **Inversion, a planted-signal market first:** agents and forecasters must recover a planted edge in a synthetic market before they touch real data, mirroring the S2 and S3 benches.
7. **Forward paper trading as the final holdout:** models are frozen and hashed, then run forward with no retraining.

**Recommendation:**
- **S4:** variations 1 + 2 + 3, with 6 and 7 as required controls.
- **S5:** variation 4 first, and variation 5 only if 4 beats the S2 portfolios. Variation 6 is required before any real-data training.

Answer the five questions (or say "defaults"), pick the variations, and tell me whether to add a Goal 8 or merge goals. Then I'll write the first idea file as a Draft.

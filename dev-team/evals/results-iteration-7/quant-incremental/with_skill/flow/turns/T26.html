full text
The two pipelines for `portfolio` are written in `docs/packages/portfolio/pipelines.md` as a Draft. Every S2 requirement lands on a step, and `dt lint` has 0 errors.

## What was written
- **PL-15: Walk-forward comparison** (`qlab-portfolio run`, part of PL-14), 10 steps:
  1. `load_returns`: one loader call per run, with gaps left missing.
  2. `build_windows`: month-end calendar, members as known on each rebalance date, the 80% rule, rows on or before the rebalance only, and months with fewer than 20 stocks skipped.
  3. `equal_weight`
  4. `analyse_structure`: fitted per window, unlike RF-01.
  5. `estimate_covariances`: first version is the sample estimator, repaired to a valid matrix.
  6. `allocate`: first version is inverse-variance weights for every method.
  7. `hold_months`: weights drift, then delisting return, then cash. Unknown payouts are listed.
  8. `score_estimators`: first version is the distance to the next month's realized covariance.
  9. `test_claims`: first version is a sign test with Bonferroni correction. Fewer than 36 months means "not tested".
  10. `write_reports`: `results.json` plus the strategy table.
- **PL-16: Structure study** (`qlab-portfolio structure`, part of PL-14). It reuses PL-15's first three steps, then `write_structure_report` (factor count over time, raw against residual correlation, no portfolios).
- **`data` contract:** `data.open_loader`, `data.Loader.panel` and `data.Loader.universe` now list `portfolio` and PL-15/PL-16 as users.
- **Planned layout:** `harness/{load,windows,holding}.py`, `structure/pca.py`, `estimators/covariance.py`, `allocators/weights.py`, `scoring/scores.py`, `claims/tests.py` and `reports/write.py`, with tests under `tests/{intent,unit}/portfolio/`.

## Assumptions
- **Every step works on all rebalances at once,** so the entry functions read as straight calls with no loops.
- **First versions are deliberately simple but real.** The thickening tasks replace them with the full estimators, the three portfolio methods, the stability and diagnostic scores, the `null-hypothesis-testing` procedure, and the `data-analysis` and `dataviz` reports. Each simple version stays available as the baseline the full one must beat.
- **Equal weight belongs to `harness`,** which already covers HARN-09. It's fed into `hold_months` alongside the other strategies.
- **`write_structure_report` returns a `RunResult`** with no months, scores or claims.
- **RF-01** is cited on `analyse_structure`, refit per window and without its zero-filling.

## Open questions
- **Module size:** `estimators/covariance.py` holds four estimators and may pass 500 lines. If it does, it gets split into one step per estimator.
- **`portfolio.cli` owns no step,** since it's the trigger. It joins the list of modules that own no step by design.

## Not covered

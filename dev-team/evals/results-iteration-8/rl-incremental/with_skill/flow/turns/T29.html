# Replan

`dt status` says `replan`: something learned during the build, or a major bug (after `pipeline-design` or `architecture-design` Revise, if a step or decision changed). Example: `examples-replan.md`.

1. Take `blocked_tasks`, the docs under `changed_since_plan` and their revised sections (`dt section <ID>`). Until those docs are re-approved, `dt status` says `blocked`: wait. Each changed `Depends on` changes its `## Dependency graph` row; a frozen interface that must change is a `contract-change`: a new interface task before every task that needs it (`phase0.md`, Frozen means frozen).

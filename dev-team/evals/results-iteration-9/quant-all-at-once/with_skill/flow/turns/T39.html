# lab — Architecture

**Package:** lab · **Status:** Approved · **Last updated:** 2026-10-17 · **System:** [../../architecture.md](../../architecture.md)

## Overview
The S2–S3 research and the final verdict. Estimators (`factors`, `denoise`, `covariance`) and constructors (`portfolios`) are fitted per `engine.Window`. `rankings` and `significance` run S2's and S3's studies, scoring weights with `engine.backtest` and every comparison inside an `engine` ledger entry. `targets` and `features` also feed S4 in `models`. `verdict` reads the ledger at the end of S4.

## Components
| Component | Responsibility | Covers | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| `factors` | Per-window PCA, parallel-analysis factor count, residual returns as of any date; FACT-03 check with the `data-analysis` skill | FACT-01–FACT-05 | `engine`, `data` |
| `denoise` | Constant-residual-eigenvalue denoising at the fitted MP edge | DENOI-01–DENOI-03 | `factors` |
| `covariance` | Estimators behind one protocol: sample, Ledoit–Wolf constant-correlation shrinkage, factor model, denoised; characterisation with the `data-analysis` skill | COV-02 | `factors`, `denoise`, `engine` |
| `portfolios` | Minimum variance, risk parity, HRP, equal weight | PORT-01, PORT-06 | `covariance` (type only), `engine` (`check_weights`) |
| `rankings` | COV and PORT ranking studies; stores the minimum-variance benchmark | COV-01, COV-03, COV-04, PORT-02–PORT-05 | `covariance`, `portfolios`, `engine` |
| `targets` | Forward log return, realised volatility and residual return over session-counted horizons, with `label_end` | SIG-01, FCST-01 | `factors`, `data` |
| `features` | Feature definitions (≥10, each with lookback and rationale), point-in-time computation, near-duplicate flags; characterisation with the `data-analysis` skill | FEAT-01–FEAT-04 | `engine`, `data` |
| `significance` | Per-feature rank-IC tests against the 5-day residual target; surviving-feature list and its gate | SIG-01–SIG-04 | `features`, `targets`, `engine` |
| `verdict` | The final report from FPORT and AGENT ledger entries, lockbox results and the benchmark | VERD-01–VERD-03 | `rankings`, `engine` |

## Interfaces
See [contract.md](contract.md).

## Data
- **Inputs:** `data.returns_panel`, `data.bars_panel`, `data.risk_free`, `data.membership_as_of`, all pinned to the registration's snapshot.
- **Benchmark:** `results/benchmark/<entry_id>.parquet`, the PORT-05 per-period minimum-variance results, read by `lab.benchmark`.
- **Correction families** (rule in AD-11): `COV` — every estimator-vs-sample comparison; `PORT` — the three risk-based-vs-equal-weight comparisons; `SIG` — every S3 feature, near-duplicates and abandoned entries included.
- **Reference code:** RF-01 for factor removal (its full-sample fit, zero-filling and fixed k replaced per AD-12); RF-03 for targets (its row-counted horizons and silent drops replaced per AD-15).

## Cross-cutting concerns
- **Errors:** `NotPositiveDefinite` (that period fails for the estimator), `WeightsError` from `engine` (including infeasible with fewer than 20 eligible stocks), `LedgerRefused` for a non-surviving feature in S4.
- **Testing:** planted-signal and pure-noise panels (FACT-02's 100 trials, DENOI-01), risk-contribution checks for PORT-06, point-in-time recomputation tests for FEAT-01.
- **Reproducibility:** each study's entry records snapshot, config hash, seed and git SHA (QR-07).

**AD-24: COV ranking order** (Proposed, affects `lab`)
- **COV ranking order:** estimators whose test passed come first, by score. Then comes `sample`, then every other estimator, by score. Ties break by name.
- **PORT ranking:** ordered by score alone, since PORT-04 ranks on variance only.
- **Score:** the mean variance over completed periods. Failed periods only affect the paired test. A method with no completed period gets a NaN score and ranks last in its group.
- **Consequence:** if no estimator passes, `sample` ranks first and S2's portfolios are built from it.
- **Contract change:** `lab.Ranking` rows gain a `failed_periods` column, so an estimator that fails often is visible beside its score.

**AD-25: what "significantly worse on the lockbox" means** (Proposed, affects `engine` and `lab`)
- `engine.score_lockbox` runs the test twice on the same lockbox data: once in the registered direction (stored as before), and once in the opposite direction, unadjusted for multiple tests. The second p-value goes in a new field, `LedgerEntry.lockbox_p_worse`.
- A method wins only if:
  - its walk-forward test passed,
  - its lockbox has been scored,
  - and `lockbox_p_worse` is 0.05 or higher.
- An entry with no lockbox score is never a winner, and its row says "not scored".
- I rejected taking 1 − p from the stored result. With an exact test on the lockbox's 24 periods, that shortcut is off by the probability of a tie at the observed value.

## S1: Data + explorer
**Outline:** Load a point-in-time S&P 500 daily dataset (prices, returns, corporate actions, membership including former members) that has been downloaded, cleaned, validated and stored, see its basic exploratory analysis, and explore it in a browser app that shows download and validation runs live.
**Skills:** data-analysis, dataviz, frontend-design

## S2: Risk structure + classical portfolios
**Outline:** Remove the market and other dominant factors by PCA, denoise the correlation matrix with Marchenko–Pastur, compare sample, shrinkage and denoised covariance on real data, then compare minimum-variance, risk parity and hierarchical risk parity on out-of-sample realized variance through a shared walk-forward evaluation engine.
**Skills:** data-analysis, null-hypothesis-testing, dataviz

## S3: Features tested against chance
**Outline:** Build candidate price and volume features and test them with ML for information gain over market-removed returns, keeping only those that pass a null-hypothesis test corrected for multiple testing; forecasting starts only if at least one survives.
**Skills:** null-hypothesis-testing, data-analysis

## S4: Forecasting + trading agents
**Outline:** Forecast returns and volatility with neural sequence models against simple baselines on the surviving features, then build forecast-driven portfolios and PPO agents in a backtest environment, each beating the S2 portfolios out of sample after costs on both Sharpe ratio and realized variance, in paper trading only.
**Skills:** null-hypothesis-testing, data-analysis

Here are the candidate workflows for S2–S4. Nothing is written until you confirm them. The routing follows what you asked for:
- `null-hypothesis-testing` goes on every "beats" or "significant" claim.
- `data-analysis` goes on every step that characterises data or compares estimators.
- `frontend-design` is already on the app (the S1 `web` pipelines and roadmap line).

**S2: return structure and classical portfolios** (`research`)

| # | Workflow | Trigger | What it does | Skills | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Equal-weight baseline | `qrp_research baseline` | Runs the shared simulator on the point-in-time universe (weekly and monthly rebalancing, 5 and 15 bp costs) and records the first scoreboard entry. This is where the backtest engine and its look-ahead test get built | — | SCORE-01–SCORE-09 |
| 2 | Factor decomposition report | `qrp_research factors` | Trailing-window PCA; components above the noise edge removed; residual returns. Reports variance share and mean correlation before and after removal | data-analysis | FACT-01–FACT-04 |
| 3 | Covariance comparison | `qrp_research cov-compare` | Sample, shrinkage and denoised estimates at each rebalance. Ranks the realised variance of each minimum-variance portfolio, by sub-period and with significance tests | data-analysis, null-hypothesis-testing | COV-01–COV-05 |
| 4 | Classical portfolios | `qrp_research portfolios` | Minimum-variance, risk parity and hierarchical risk parity, each with each estimator (9 combinations), as scoreboard entries. Pairwise "beats" claims tested and corrected | null-hypothesis-testing | PORT-01–PORT-04, SCORE-10 |
| 5 | S2 held-out verdict | `qrp_research verdict S2` | Pre-register, unlock once, score the pre-registered portfolios against equal weight on the held-out years | null-hypothesis-testing | PORT-05, HOLD-02–HOLD-05 |

**S3: features and significance** (`research`)

| # | Workflow | Trigger | What it does | Skills | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Register a feature | `qrp_research feature add` | Adds a definition, lookback and date to the catalogue before it can be tested | — | FEAT-01 |
| 7 | Feature significance run | `qrp_research features test` | Computes features and residual targets at 1, 5 and 21 days (delisting returns kept). Measures information gain with and without each feature on purged walk-forward folds, tests each gain, corrects the false discovery rate across the whole catalogue, and records passes and failures | null-hypothesis-testing | FEAT-02–FEAT-04, SIG-01–SIG-04 |
| 8 | Planted-signal check | `qrp_research features calibrate` | Runs 100 noise features and 1 planted one through the same test: the planted one must pass, and the noise must stay within the 10% false discovery rate | null-hypothesis-testing | SIG-05 |

**S4: forecasting and trading agents** (`models`)

| # | Workflow | Trigger | What it does | Skills | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Build training windows | `qrp_models windows` | Windows indexed by trading day, built only from features that passed S3, with an embargo and encoders fitted on training data only | — | FCST-01, FCST-04 |
| 10 | Train a forecaster | `qrp_models train [--gpu]` | LSTM or temporal-fusion model trained over 5 or more seeds, on the laptop or in a rented GPU session (anonymised bundle, then wipe and terminate) | — | FCST-06, QR-08, QR-09 |
| 11 | Forecasters vs baselines | `qrp_models evaluate` | Volatility loss and return rank correlation against the simple baselines at each horizon; claims corrected across every forecaster, baseline and horizon | null-hypothesis-testing | FCST-02, FCST-03, FCST-05 |
| 12 | Forecast-driven portfolios | `qrp_models fport` | Only forecasters that beat their baselines, combined with the best S2 covariance estimate, as scoreboard entries | — | FPORT-01, FPORT-02 |
| 13 | Train and score PPO agents | `qrp_models agents` | Gymnasium wrapper over the shared simulator (checked against the backtest to within 1e-10), trained on development data over 5 or more seeds, as scoreboard entries | — | AGENT-01–AGENT-05 |
| 14 | Final verdict | `qrp_models verdict` | Pre-register, unlock once, compare the S4 candidates against the S2 candidates on Sharpe after costs, with volatility, turnover and drawdown at both cost levels | null-hypothesis-testing | VERD-01–VERD-03 |

All 14 cover the 32 S2–S3 IDs left over from S1, plus every S4 ID. Each test claim names the `null-hypothesis-testing` skill rather than defining its own procedure, so the skill picks the statistic and the null distribution.

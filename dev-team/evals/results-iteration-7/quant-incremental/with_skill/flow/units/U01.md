## Recommended combinations

**A. Sharadar Prices annual + Tiingo + fja05680. About $24.92/mo, or $49.92/mo with Tiingo Power.**
- **Primary:** Sharadar SEP, starting 2006, with delisted names, `permaticker`, the actions table and unadjusted closes.
- **Membership:** Sharadar's S&P 500 table, cross-checked against fja05680.
- **Second source:** Tiingo raw `close`. The free tier's 500 symbols a month means pulling the roughly 900–1,000 tickers of S&P 500 members since 2011 (my estimate) over two or three months. Power at $25/mo (annual) gets it done in one day.
- **Fails:** delisting returns and announcement dates.
- **Partly meets:** whether Tiingo reaches 80% of member-days hinges on its delisted coverage, which I couldn't verify. Matching Tiingo's tickers to Sharadar's needs date-range checks, because reused tickers can map to the wrong company.

**B. Sharadar Prices annual + EODHD Historian annual. About $41.50/mo ($24.92 + $16.58).**
- **Second source:** EODHD's raw `close`, with 60k delisted US symbols and a bulk API. This is the most likely to clear 80% of member-days.
- **Fails:** delisting returns and announcement dates.
- **Partly meets:** EODHD has no permanent ID, so reused tickers have to be resolved through Sharadar's ticker date ranges.

**C. Fallback without Sharadar: EODHD Historian + EODHD constituents add-on + Tiingo free + fja05680. About $46.57/mo, or $50 for the add-on's first three months.**
- **Fails:** permanent ID, delisting reason and return, and announcement dates.
- **Partly meets:** official membership history only goes back about 12 years (roughly 2014), so 2006–2014 depends on the free fja05680 list alone.
- This is clearly weaker than A or B.

**Norgate Platinum** would make sense only if you can stretch the budget to about $53/mo for it alone, run it on Windows or in a Windows VM, and accept that you lose all the data when the subscription ends.

Before buying, confirm these with a free trial or sample: what Sharadar's `value` field and delisting-reason codes contain; whether Sharadar records announcement dates anywhere; Tiingo's delisted coverage; EODHD's terms on local storage; and FMP's tier and depth for its S&P 500 history.

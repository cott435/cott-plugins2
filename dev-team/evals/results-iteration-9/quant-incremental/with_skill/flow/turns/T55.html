## The six contract changes (`docs/packages/qfcst/contract.md`)
1. **`dataset.Survivors`:** the list ID, the surviving feature names in the list's order, and `source` set to `"holdout"`, or `"none"` when nothing passed or the run was procedure-suspect.
   - `dataset.survivors` returns it.
   - `build_dataset` and `ForecastSetup.survivors` take it.
2. **`evaluate.DevEvaluation`:** per-date scores (rank correlation for return, QLIKE for volatility) plus the 3 development claims. `evaluate_dev` returns it.
3. **`evaluate.ErrorInputs`:** four development-only tables:
   - scores by 5-year block;
   - scores by volatility-baseline tercile;
   - calibration by decile;
   - missing share per year.

   `error_inputs` returns it.
4. **`engine.BacktestReport`:** one row per portfolio with return, volatility, Sharpe after costs, turnover, cost and days, always labelled "development, not a verdict". `backtest` returns it.
5. **`evaluate.DevRun`:** a write-once record of a development run (ID, version, survivors, config, claims, folder), with new `write_dev_run` and `load_dev_run` functions. `SetupRegistry.register(dev, review)` now takes the list, survivors, config and claims from the `DevRun`. It takes the eight rules, 5 bp, the four comparisons and the January retraining from `config/forecasts.toml`.
6. **`book.BookReturns`:** sessions × the eight portfolios' daily returns after costs over the 12 complete months, read only from the book's records.

## Also in the root architecture
- **AD-12, revised in place:** its scope adds `qfcst` (7 pipelines sharing one dataset, model, engine and book, with PyTorch in one module). Its rejected alternatives add splitting out the paper book, and it lists the three contracts kept whole.
- **Changelog:** one new row.

There's no new decision: these are interface additions with no technical choice behind them. Their consumers are the `qfcst` pipelines you just approved, which already use these shapes, so nothing goes back to pipeline-design.

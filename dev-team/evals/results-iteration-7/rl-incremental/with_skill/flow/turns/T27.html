I've written the architecture extension as a draft. All gate checks pass: nothing is uncovered, nothing changed is left unabsorbed, and lint shows 0 errors. The contract is now 576 lines.

**Shape:** a single package, **through S3** (AD-12, revised in place). No fourth environment is planned, so the split question only comes back if you propose one. The named seams now include `forage` with the environments, `selfplay` with the agents, and `xplay` with the lab.

**New components**
- **`forage`:** the foraging tank. It reuses the tank's geometry and adds pellets, teams and catches; each fish sees 50 values, and each team gets a score.
  - `ForageConfig` carries every size from FORAGE-01 to FORAGE-12, and its predator speed is the setting the sweep varies.
  - `ForageEnv.teams` is the fixed row order: three prey, then the predator.
  - It has a `set_state` test hook (AD-11) and uses the new shape roles `pellet`, `prey` and `predator`.
- **`selfplay`:** `TeamConfig` (the schedule, who learns, the scripted opponents, alternate every 10 updates, snapshot every 50,000 steps, a pool of 20) and `TeamAgent`, which:
  - routes each row to its side's policy and lets only the sides the schedule marks as learning update;
  - keeps its snapshots in memory and saves them all in `team.pt`;
  - offers `versus(side, opponent)` for evaluating against a reference.
- **`xplay`:** `cross_play(run)` builds the reference curves, the S × S matrices and both forgetting scores, and writes `xplay.json`. `forgetting_score` is a pure function, so the 0.5 example can be tested by hand.

**Smaller changes**
- **`baselines`:** a new `ChaserAgent`, and the flee fish reads foraging observations (where the predator is the threat, and pellets are the target).
- **`evaluate` and `RunConfig`:** both gain a `side` setting; with it, an episode's score is that team's score (EVAL-05).
- **`train`:** a team run's final evaluation scores each side against its scripted reference. On Ctrl-C it now saves the agent, snapshots included, before closing the run.
- **`compare`:** gains `write_forgetting_comparison`, and its comparisons can now take the alternative "less".
- **`plots`:** gains `crossplay_heatmap`.
- **`experiments`:** adds the agent names `chaser` and `team`, the environment `forage`, and six new presets (five schedules plus the speed sweep).
- **`cli`:** gains an `xplay` subcommand.

**Decisions**
- **AD-12**, revised in place: single package through S3.
- **AD-04**, revised in place: `selfplay` may also import PyTorch.
- **New AD-16, self-play:** one two-team agent, schedules as a setting, snapshots kept in memory and saved with the agent. Frozen snapshots sample from their policy; scripted opponents are deterministic.
- **New AD-17, measurement:** cross-play runs **after** training, on saved snapshots, with both policies batched and episodes that end "cleared" stopping early.

**Risks, merged into the one list**
- Self-play might collapse or cycle badly enough to fail the bars. That's partly the lesson.
- **Cross-play is costly:** up to 2,000 episodes per run, across 10 runs. Batching should keep it to minutes; if not, the fallback is a spec change to fewer episodes per cell.
- 5 vs 5 runs gives the forgetting comparison little statistical power.
- The resolved "M1 predates AD-13" risk is removed.

**Assumptions:** `ForageEnv.teams` is an extra attribute, outside the core protocol, which the team agent reads. A foraging episode's `info["score"]` is the prey score. A foraging run's `final_eval.json` holds both sides' scores.

**One effect on M1:** `core.Shape` gains the three new roles. It's a one-line extension to an interface M1 freezes, which is why it's added now, before the build. Once you approve, the planning tool will flag `core.Shape` as changed since the plan was committed. I'll handle that with a one-line replan note and a re-commit, with no task changes. M1 then stays ready for `/build`.

2. test-planning: golden cases for pellets, catches, rays and team scores, plus the forgetting score and schedule maths;
3. the S3 bar extension at its kickoff (the `forage`, `selfplay` and `xplay` import layers).

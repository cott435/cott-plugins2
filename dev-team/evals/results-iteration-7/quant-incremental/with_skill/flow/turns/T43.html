## S4: `docs/ideas/forecasts-and-portfolios.md` (about 770 words)
- **Problem:** do forecasts, turned into portfolios, beat S2 per unit of risk after costs? Every past period has been looked at, so only future months are an untouched test.
- **Direction:**
  - **One monthly backtest environment** with 10 basis points of cost per side, where S2's strategies, equal weight and every forecast portfolio compete.
  - **Volatility forecasts first:** EWMA, HAR and GARCH, then neural sequence models.
  - **Then return forecasts:** zero, historical mean and S3's ridge, then neural models. Inputs are raw histories plus S3 survivors.
  - **Forecasts into portfolios:** mean-variance with shrinkage, and volatility-scaled S2 portfolios.
  - **Judging:** Sharpe after costs against S2's best strategy (named beforehand) and equal weight. Every claim is a paired monthly test through `null-hypothesis-testing`, corrected in S3's ledger, with deflated Sharpe ratios.
  - **Confirmation:** prospective paper trading, run by hand after each monthly update.
  - **Tools and prior art:** PyTorch, with RF-02 and RF-03 as prior art, minus their flaws.
- **Biggest Not doing:** no RL agents (that's S5) and no automated paper-trading service.
- **Open questions for its spec:**
  - How is S2's "best" named in advance?
  - Which neural architectures and tuning grids are in the fixed comparison set?
  - Does forecast uncertainty enter the portfolios?

## S5: `docs/ideas/ppo-agents.md` (about 550 words)
- **Problem:** RL is sample-hungry, unstable across seeds, and overfits backtests. A lucky seed or a leaky environment looks brilliant.
- **Direction:**
  - **Same arena:** S4's environment, costs, competitors and measures.
  - **Correctness first:** hand-written PPO checked against an established implementation before any market result counts.
  - **Honest reporting:** results reported across many seeds, never the best seed.
  - **Leak test:** perturb future data, and the agent's actions must not change.
  - **Claims:** through `null-hypothesis-testing`, corrected in the ledger, with deflated Sharpe ratios.
  - **Confirmation:** prospective paper trading beside S4's.
- **Biggest Not doing:** no RL algorithm other than PPO.
- **Open questions for its spec:**

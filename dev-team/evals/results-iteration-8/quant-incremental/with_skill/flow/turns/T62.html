The spec is Approved (181 requirements), and both ideas are Specced and frozen, with their open questions marked "Resolved in the spec." The roadmap shows S4 and S5 as Specced, and lint is clean.

Next is the architecture Extend for S4 and S5, which places 46 requirements that nothing covers yet. One decision has to come from you first, because it's expensive to reverse.

**A Grow trigger fires:** S4 and S5 bring a deep-learning stack (PyTorch plus a reinforcement-learning library). None of the existing packages should carry it, so they need a new home.

**Options:**
- **A. Two new packages (recommended).**
  - **`qcast` (S4):** forecasts, forecast portfolios, the planted forecast market, net-of-cost evaluation and the model log, forward paper trading, and the shared report. It reads `qdata`, `qrisk` (windows, estimators, portfolios) and `qfeat` (confirmed features).
  - **`qagent` (S5):** the backtest environment, the agents and the planted environments. It depends on `qcast` for forecasts, the forecast-portfolio sleeve, the evaluation and the model log.
  - **How the arrows stay one-way:** paper trading and the report live in `qcast`, so `qagent` hands its frozen policies and results to `qcast` through a small published interface. This keeps `qcast` from ever importing `qagent`, at the cost of one small interface; that would be its own decision record.
- **B. One package `qlearn` for both stages.** Simpler wiring, but about 8 pipelines in one package. That trips the same guideline as `qrisk` and `qfeat` again, and the S5 RL library would land in S4's package.
- **C. Three packages:** A, plus a separate `qpaper` for forward paper trading and the report, depending on both. This is the cleanest dependency direction, but one more package to maintain for a thin layer.

**What I'll recommend in the draft, unless you object now:**
- **PyTorch on Apple Silicon (MPS)** for both the forecaster and the agents, kept out of every existing package.
- **The forecaster written by hand:** a compact temporal-fusion-style model following your RF-02 and RF-03 references, with their leakage caveats fixed. An established library implementation would serve as a test-only reference, the same way PyPortfolioOpt does for HRP.
- **PPO written by hand** in the single-file style, with a mature RL library as a test-only reference.

Which shape do you want? A is the default.

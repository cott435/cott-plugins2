requirementBASE-01Each world has a random agent that picks uniformly among its allowed actions.
criteria

criteria

criteria
  • Given the several-fish version, when the scripted fish observes, then a ball beyond 0.5 is hidden from it as in TANK-07.

featureSNAKESnake world
stagesS1
stage_viaStage line
source—

requirementSNAKE-01The world is a flat 2D 10 × 10 grid with the snake starting 3 cells long in the middle, heading right.
criteria
  • Given a new episode, when it starts, then the snake is 3 cells long, its head is within one cell of the centre, and it is heading right.
requirementSNAKE-02Exactly one food item is on the board, on a random empty cell.
criteria
  • Given food has just been eaten, when the next step starts, then one new food item is on a cell the snake does not occupy.
requirementSNAKE-03Each step the agent chooses one of three moves: turn left, go straight, or turn right.
criteria
  • Given the snake is heading right, when the agent chooses "turn left", then the head moves one cell up.
requirementSNAKE-04Eating food lengthens the snake by one cell.
criteria
  • Given the head moves onto the food, when the step ends, then the snake is one cell longer and the episode's score rises by 1.
requirementSNAKE-05Hitting a wall or the snake's own body ends the episode.
criteria
  • Given the head moves off the grid, when the step ends, then the episode is over.
  • Given the head moves onto a body cell, when the step ends, then the episode is over.
requirementSNAKE-06An episode with 100 steps in a row without eating ends, so a looping snake can't run forever.
criteria
  • Given the snake has gone 100 steps since it last ate, when the 100th step ends, then the episode is over with its score so far.
requirementSNAKE-07The episode score is the number of food items eaten.
criteria
  • Given a snake that ate 7 items and then hit a wall, when the episode ends, then its score is 7.

featureTANKFish tank world
stagesS2
stage_viaStage line
source—
servesG4
requirementTANK-01The tank is a flat 2D circle of radius 1 (tank units); nothing leaves it.
criteria
  • Given any fish or the ball, when any step ends, then it is inside the circle.
requirementTANK-02The ball moves in a straight line at a constant speed of 0.02 per step and bounces off the tank wall like a mirror, losing no speed.
criteria
  • Given the ball reaches the wall, when the step ends, then its angle of travel is reflected about the wall's normal and its speed is still 0.02.
requirementTANK-03Each step a fish chooses a continuous heading and a continuous speed from 0 up to 0.015 per step (three quarters of the ball's speed).
criteria
  • Given a fish chooses heading 90° and speed 0.01, when the step ends, then it has moved 0.01 in that direction (unless stopped by the wall).
requirementTANK-04A fish the ball touches is out for the rest of the episode.
criteria
  • Given the ball's edge (radius 0.1) overlaps a fish's edge (radius 0.03), when the step ends, then that fish is out and its survival time is the steps it lasted.
requirementTANK-05In the first version, one fish sees the whole world: its own position and movement, and the ball's position and movement.
criteria
  • Given the single-fish version, when the fish observes, then it receives the ball's position and velocity wherever the ball is.
requirementTANK-06In the second version, 5 fish swim in the tank at once.
criteria
  • Given the several-fish version, when an episode starts, then 5 fish are in the tank.
requirementTANK-07In the second version, each fish sees only things within 0.5 of itself (a quarter of the tank's width): the ball and other fish beyond that distance are hidden from it.
criteria
  • Given the ball is 0.6 from a fish, when that fish observes, then the ball is absent from its observation.
  • Given the ball is 0.4 from a fish, when that fish observes, then the ball's position and velocity relative to the fish are in its observation.
requirementTANK-08An episode ends after 1,000 steps or when every fish is out.
criteria
  • Given one fish survives to step 1,000, when step 1,000 ends, then the episode is over and that fish's survival time is 1,000.
requirementTANK-09The episode score is the mean survival time, in steps, of the fish in it.
criteria
  • Given 5 fish lasting 200, 400, 600, 800 and 1,000 steps, when the episode ends, then its score is 600.

featureCOEVOCo-evolution comparison
stagesS3
stage_viaStage line
source—
servesG4, G1
requirementCOEVO-01In the simultaneous regime, both sides learn at once, each always facing the other's current version.
criteria
  • Given a simultaneous run, when the predator updates, then the prey it trained against were the prey's latest version at that moment.
requirementCOEVO-02In the past-versions regime, a snapshot of each side is saved at least every 10% of the run, and each side trains against opponents drawn from all the other side's snapshots so far.
criteria
  • Given a past-versions run at 50% progress, when the predator plays a training episode, then its prey come from one of the prey's 5 or more saved snapshots.
requirementCOEVO-03Both regimes are compared side by side: every regime's final predator plays every regime's final prey for 100 evaluation episodes.
criteria
  • Given both finished regimes, when compared, then a 2 × 2 table of predator and prey scores is produced.
requirementCOEVO-04Both regimes' learning curves are drawn on one chart with the dataviz skill, predator and prey shown separately.
criteria
  • Given both finished regimes, when the chart is drawn, then it shows four curves: each regime's predator and prey.
requirementCOEVO-05Each final agent plays the opponent's saved snapshots in order, showing whether later versions do better against it than earlier ones.
criteria
  • Given the final predator from either regime, when played against each prey snapshot for 100 episodes, then its catch rate per snapshot is reported in order.
requirementCOEVO-06Differences between the regimes are tested with the null-hypothesis-testing skill at 5%, corrected for the number of comparisons.
criteria
  • Given the 2 × 2 table's scores, when tested, then each regime difference is reported with its corrected p-value.

requirementPREY-07The prey score is pellets eaten per prey per episode; the predator score is prey caught per episode.
criteria

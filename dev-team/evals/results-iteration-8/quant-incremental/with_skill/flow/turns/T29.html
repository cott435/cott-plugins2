You've picked the direction, so I'll go straight to the stress test and then write. You asked for six goals explicitly, so I'll add Goal 6 rather than merging two.

**Stress test, recorded in the idea file:**
- **Dimensions:** 500 names with a 2-year window gives a ratio of about 1. The sample covariance is then barely invertible, and the Marchenko–Pastur edge sits at about (1 + √1)² = 4. That is exactly where denoising and shrinkage should show their worth. Long-only with a 5% cap also regularizes the optimizer. The 1-year check (ratio about 2) is a stress test, and the top-100 fallback stays in reserve.
- **Point-in-time traps:**
  - **Names without a full window:** members that joined less than 2 years before a rebalance don't have a complete window. Excluding them biases toward old names; including them needs gap handling.
  - **Delisting mid-month:** names that delist inside a holding month need a rule, given the brief's bet that there's no final-day return.
- **The significance claim** goes to the **null-hypothesis-testing** skill, and the **eigenvalue spectra** and residual-correlation analysis to **data-analysis**. Both are installed, and both describe exactly these jobs.

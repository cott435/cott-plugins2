### AD-07: Returns, identity and missing data rules
**Status:** Proposed · **Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Scope:** `qdata`
- **Context:** LOAD-01 (close, split-adjusted close, total return), LOAD-05 and BUILD-03 (missing stays missing), UNIV-03 and FCST-06 (final returns through delisting), DATA-03 (no split jumps).
- **Decision:** Total return on day t is `closeadj_t / closeadj_prev − 1`, where prev is the stock's previous trading day on the exchange calendar (`exchange_calendars` XNYS). A day with no trade is NaN, and the next return spans from the last traded close with `gap_days` recorded. The final return through delisting is the return to the last traded close. Identity is `permaticker`, and loaded frames carry the ticker as of each date as a column.
- **Alternatives:** Filling missing days with zero returns: forbidden by LOAD-05. Computing total returns from actions ourselves: duplicates the vendor's adjustment and is checked instead by XCHK-03.
- **Consequences:** Delisting returns after the last trade (a cash payout or a worthless delisting) aren't in Sharadar's prices, so they're missing (Risks).
- **Probe:** PB-01 — reused from AD-03: confirms the `closeadj` behaviour and whether the actions table carries delisting values.

### AD-20: The benchmark is the lowest-variance candidate, equal weight included
**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Scope:** `research`, `learn`
- **Context:** PORT-06 saves the portfolio with the lowest out-of-sample variance after costs as the stage benchmark, and PORT-05 runs equal weight alongside under the same schedule and cost. `research.Benchmark.estimator` allowed only the three estimators, so an equal-weight winner had no value (TC-34). UR-52: the user chose the lowest-variance candidate after costs, equal weight included, with the estimator optional. FPORT-01 still needs the best `COV` estimator when the benchmark has none.
- **Decision:** `select_benchmark` ranks all ten S2 candidates (three methods × three estimators, plus equal weight) on mean realized variance after costs, ties broken by registration order. `Benchmark.estimator` is `None` exactly when the winner is equal weight. `Benchmark.best_estimator` is always set: the estimator of the lowest-variance candidate that has one, the same as `estimator` when the winner has one. Forecast-driven portfolios take their covariance from `best_estimator`, and the paper benchmark book holds equal weight with no covariance when `method == "equal"`.
- **Alternatives:** Limit the benchmark to the nine estimator-based candidates: later stages could then beat a benchmark that equal weight already beats. Use equal weight's estimator as a sentinel string: a value that names no estimator, which every consumer would have to special-case.
- **Consequences:** `benchmarks/S2.json` stores `method`, `estimator` (null for equal weight) and `best_estimator`. PL-21's forecast portfolios and PL-24's benchmark book read the new fields.
- **Probe:** none — an internal type, no external service.

## Risks and open questions
- **PB-01 (AD-03):** the Sharadar subscription may not support the dollar-volume universe from 2000, delisted names included. Then UNIV-04's S&P 500 fallback applies. The probe answers before `pipeline-design` starts on `qdata`.
- **PB-02 (AD-04):** Yahoo coverage of delisted stocks may be low. XCHK-04 reports it, but if covered stock-days are too few for XCHK-01 to mean anything, the user decides whether that's acceptable.
- **PB-03 (AD-05):** the sp500 table may not be in the subscription. Paying for it would need the user's yes under QR-04.
- **Known dates before the first build (AD-03, AD-06):** Sharadar keeps only the latest value of each row. Rows loaded by the first build are taken as known on their trading date, so as-of reads before 2026-10 can't see corrections made before then. The build report states this.
- **PB-01 (AD-07):** AD-07 stays Proposed until PB-01 confirms the price and delisting columns.
- **Delisting returns after the last trade (AD-07):** cash-out or worthless delistings aren't in the price table, so the last return is the one to the last traded close. This may overstate returns for failed companies. PB-01 checks whether the actions table carries delisting values.
- **research contract size:** `research`'s contract is 227 lines, over the 200-line ceiling, and `learn`, `server` and `web` each have more than three pipelines. Splitting them (Grow) is deferred by the user's choice of 2026-10-18.
- **Yahoo endpoints are unofficial (AD-04):** a change on Yahoo's side would mark the cross-check "not run" until the adapter is fixed. The build can still be accepted by hand.

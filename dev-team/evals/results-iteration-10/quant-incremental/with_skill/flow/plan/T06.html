requirementCHECK-01A validation run checks 100 members that the independent source still carries, sampled across 1998 to today.
criteria
  • Given a validation run, when it finishes, then it has compared exactly 100 stocks (or all available, if fewer), and every calendar year from 1998 to today has at least 2 stocks that were members in that year.
requirementCHECK-02For each sampled stock, the run also checks every split and special dividend that stock had.
criteria
  • Given a sampled stock with 3 splits and 1 special dividend since 1998, when the run finishes, then all 4 events have been compared.
requirementCHECK-03A full validation run, on demand, checks every member the independent source carries.
criteria
  • Given a full validation run, when it finishes, then every member the independent source carries has been compared.
requirementCHECK-04Splits agree only when the date and the ratio match exactly.
criteria
  • Given a 3-for-2 split on one source and a 2-for-1 split on the same date on the other, when compared, then a finding is raised.
requirementCHECK-05Dividends agree only when the ex-date matches and the amount is within $0.001.
criteria
  • Given amounts of $0.2500 and $0.2509 on the same ex-date, when compared, then they agree; given $0.2500 and $0.2515, then a finding is raised.
requirementCHECK-06Daily total returns agree when they are within 5 basis points (0.05 percentage points) of each other.
criteria
  • Given returns of +1.20% and +1.24% for the same stock and day, when compared, then they agree; given +1.20% and +1.26%, then a finding is raised.
requirementCHECK-07Unadjusted closes agree when they are within 0.5% of each other.
criteria
  • Given closes of $40.00 and $40.18, when compared, then they agree; given $40.00 and $40.25, then a finding is raised.
requirementCHECK-08A stock the independent source doesn't carry is checked for missing trading days before its last trading day.
criteria
  • Given a delisted stock with no price on a day when the exchange was open and the stock was not halted, when checked, then a finding is raised for that day.
requirementCHECK-09A stock acquired for a known cash price is checked for a last close consistent with the deal.
criteria
  • Given a cash acquisition at $70.00 a share and a last close of $69.95, when checked, then it passes; given a last close more than 2% from the deal price, then a finding is raised.
requirementCHECK-10A stock's index membership is checked to end no later than its last trading day.
criteria
  • Given a stock whose membership ends after its last trading day, when checked, then a finding is raised.
requirementCHECK-11Findings from internal checks are listed and resolved in the same way as disagreements with the independent source.
criteria
  • Given a CHECK-08 finding and a CHECK-06 finding, when findings are listed, then both appear in one list with the same fields and the same resolve actions.
requirementCHECK-12Each finding records the stock, the date(s), the check that raised it, both values, and the difference.
criteria
  • Given any finding, when viewed, then all five are shown.
requirementCHECK-13The researcher resolves a finding by marking it explained, with a written reason, or accepted.
criteria
  • Given an open finding, when marked explained with no reason, then it is refused; with a reason, then its status becomes explained and the reason and date are kept.
requirementCHECK-14A resolved finding stays resolved in later runs while both values are unchanged.
criteria
  • Given an explained finding, when the next run finds the same difference, then no new finding is raised; when either value has changed, then the finding is reopened.
requirementCHECK-15The 10 named former members are present with both end dates matching the reference table in DATA-04.
criteria

requirementRET-01For every stock that was investable on any date since 1998, the system returns its daily total-return series between two given dates.
criteria
  • Given any date D since 1998-01-02 and the universe for D, when total returns from D to D + 20 trading days are requested, then a series is returned for every member, including members that stopped trading in that window.
requirementRET-02A daily total return combines the price change and any cash dividend going ex on that day.
criteria
  • Given a stock with a $0.50 dividend going ex on day T, a close of $50.00 on T − 1 and a close of $49.75 on T, when returns are requested, then the return for T is (49.75 + 0.50) / 50.00 − 1 = +0.50%.
requirementRET-03Daily total returns are unaffected by stock splits.
criteria
  • Given a 2-for-1 split effective on day T with closes of $100.00 on T − 1 and $50.50 on T, when returns are requested, then the return for T is +1.00%.
requirementRET-04Results requested as of date D use no information published after D.
criteria
  • Given a split or dividend that goes ex after D, when returns or prices are requested as of D, then the values up to D are identical to those produced before that event was loaded.
requirementRET-05Price levels are available unadjusted, or adjusted only for splits and dividends that went ex on or before the as-of date.
criteria
  • Given a 2-for-1 split on day S > D, when adjusted prices are requested as of D, then they equal the unadjusted prices for every day up to D.
requirementRET-06A stock's return series runs through its last trading day, including the return on that last day.
criteria
  • Given Lehman Brothers, when returns through 2008-09-30 are requested, then the return for 2008-09-15 is present and below −90%.
  • Given a company acquired for cash, when returns are requested, then the return on its last trading day is present and the series ends that day.
requirementRET-07Every returned series states whether each day is a trading day with a price, or a gap.
criteria
  • Given a trading halt with no close on day T, when returns are requested, then day T is marked as a gap and has no invented return.

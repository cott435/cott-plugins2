I've drafted the S2–S4 test plans: 34 new golden cases (TC-32 to TC-65), with fixtures and expected outputs. Every pipeline now has a smoke test and at least one golden case, and lint shows zero errors.

Writing these cases surfaced one problem that needs your decision.

**COV-03 can't hold for the sample covariance on the real universe.** COV-03 says every covariance estimate must be positive definite. With about 500 names and a 252-day window, there are more stocks than days, so the sample covariance is singular (its smallest eigenvalue is 0). The 504-day sensitivity window only just clears 500 names, and membership turnover pushes the count above that.

- TC-39 uses 100 names, so it passes and doesn't catch this.
- **My recommendation:** give the sample estimate a declared tiny ridge (1e-6 × the mean variance) and label it as such, so the requirement holds for all three methods.
- **Alternative:** limit COV-03 to the shrinkage and denoised estimates.

Either way it's a spec change (COV-03) and a small architecture change (AD-08). Which do you want?

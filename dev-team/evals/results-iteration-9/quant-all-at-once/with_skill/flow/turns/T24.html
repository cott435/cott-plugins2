### AD-08: Returns built from unadjusted prices, actions and the exchange calendar
**Status:** Proposed · **Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Scope:** `data`
- **Context:** VALID-05 needs adjusted returns equal to economic total return within 0.01 pp; PANEL-03 needs delisting returns (cash payout, or −100% flagged estimated); PANEL-04 forbids filled gaps; DATA-05 and VALID-03 need the exchange calendar. RF-03 counts horizons in rows, which spec FCST-01 forbids.
- **Decision:** Daily total return = (unadjusted close × split factor + cash dividend) / previous unadjusted close − 1, with spin-offs taken from Sharadar's `closeadj` ratio on the ex-date; VALID-05 cross-checks every action day against `closeadj`. Delisting return: the recorded payout or final price versus the last close; with neither, −100% and `estimated=True`. Trading days come from `exchange_calendars` XNYS; a date where SEP has bars for most members but XNYS says closed (or the reverse) fails validation.
- **Alternatives:** Use `closeadj` returns directly — no independent check of VALID-05 and no delisting payouts. A hand-maintained holiday list — error-prone for special closures.
- **Consequences:** Every return in the platform comes from `data.returns_panel`; horizons everywhere count XNYS sessions (AD-15).
- **Probe:** PB-01 — whether ACTIONS gives payouts determines how many delisting returns are `estimated`.

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Scope:** `server`, `app`
- **Context:** QR-04 (local-only), QR-06 (each shape declared once and checked on both sides), QR-08 (≤2 s stock page, ≤1 s membership) and RUNS-01 (≤2 s live updates).

requirementCHECK-01The learning algorithms (how actions are chosen, what is learned from each experience, and how the agent is updated) are written by the learner; automatic gradient computation may be used, but an established reference implementation of a learning method is used only for this check, never inside training runs.
criteria
  • Given any training run, when it runs, then no outside reinforcement-learning code takes part in it.
requirementCHECK-02For each world and variant, 5 runs of a reference implementation of the same kind of method are trained on the same world, budget and seeds as the hand-written method, and evaluated as in EVAL-03.
criteria
  • Given a finished reference check, when its summary is opened, then it shows the reference's mean and range next to the hand-written method's.
requirementCHECK-03The hand-written method passes the check if its mean final score is at least 80% of the reference's (or at most 125% for lower-is-better scores).
criteria
  • Given a hand-written snake mean of 18 food and a reference mean of 20, when checked, then it passes; at 15 against 20, it is flagged for investigation.
featureEVALBaseline evaluation
stagesS1
stage_viaStage line
source—
servesG1
requirementEVAL-01Every world and variant has a random-play baseline that picks each action uniformly from the allowed actions.
criteria
  • Given any world, when the random baseline plays 100 steps, then every action it takes is within the declared action format.
requirementEVAL-02Every world and variant has a rule-based baseline that the learner writes by hand, described in one sentence in the world's feature below.
criteria
  • Given any world, when its rule baseline is listed, then its one-sentence description is shown with it.
requirementEVAL-03Final evaluation plays every final checkpoint and both baselines on the same 100 seeds, none of which was used in training or in-training evaluation.
criteria
  • Given 5 finished runs, when final evaluation is run, then 700 episodes are played (5 × 100 learned, 100 random, 100 rule-based), all on the same 100 held-out seeds.
requirementEVAL-04Whether the learned agent beats each baseline is tested with the null-hypothesis-testing skill. It is given: the hypothesis "the trained agent scores better than the baseline"; H0 = the learned agent's mean score is no better than the baseline's, H1 = it is better (in the world's score direction); the statistic = difference in mean episode score, learned (averaged over the runs, per seed) minus baseline; the null built by randomly swapping the learned and baseline labels within each held-out seed (paired permutation, 10,000 permutations); α = 0.05 with Holm correction across all baseline comparisons in that world (2 per variant); the EVAL-03 held-out episodes. The learned agent passes only if both nulls are rejected.
criteria
  • Given final evaluation results for one variant, when tested, then a report shows, for each baseline, the mean scores, the difference, the corrected p-value and reject/keep.
requirementEVAL-05"Clearly beats" also needs every single run to beat the rule baseline: each run's final mean score is better than the rule-based baseline's mean score.
criteria
  • Given 5 runs where 4 beat the rule baseline and 1 does not, when the verdict is made, then the world is marked "not yet beating the baselines" and names the failing run.
requirementEVAL-06Final evaluation produces one summary per world and variant: mean and range of learned scores across runs, both baseline means, the EVAL-04 test results and the EVAL-05 verdict.
criteria
  • Given a finished evaluation, when the summary is opened, then all of those values are present.

requirementSNAKE-01The world is a 10 × 10 grid with one snake starting 3 cells long in the middle, and one food item on a random free cell.
criteria
  • Given seed 3, when an episode starts, then the snake is 3 cells long near the center and one food item is on a cell the snake does not cover.
requirementSNAKE-02Each step the snake chooses one of three actions: turn left, turn right or go straight.
criteria
  • Given the snake heading up, when it turns left, then its head moves one cell left.
requirementSNAKE-03Eating food grows the snake by one cell, adds 1 to the score and places new food on a random free cell.
criteria
  • Given the head moves onto food, when the step ends, then the snake is one cell longer, the score is up by 1 and new food is on a free cell.
requirementSNAKE-04The episode ends when the head hits a wall or the snake's body, or after 100 steps without eating.
criteria
  • Given the head moves into its own body, when the step ends, then the episode is over.
requirementSNAKE-05The score is the food eaten in the episode (higher is better).
criteria
  • Given an episode where 12 food items were eaten, when it ends, then its score is 12.
requirementSNAKE-06The snake observes a fixed list of numbers: danger straight ahead, left and right; its heading; the food's direction relative to its head; and its length.
criteria
  • Given any step, when the observation is taken, then it has the same length and holds those values.
requirementSNAKE-07The rule-based baseline moves toward the food along the shorter axis, unless that move would end the episode next step, in which case it takes any move that does not.
criteria
  • Given the food to the right and a free cell to the right, when the rule acts, then it moves right.
requirementSNAKE-08The trained snake beats both baselines as in EVAL-04 and EVAL-05.
criteria
  • Given 5 finished snake runs, when evaluated, then both baselines are beaten.

requirementTRAIN-01A training run is started from a world, a variant, the method's settings and a seed, and runs for a stated budget of environment steps.
criteria
  • Given snake, its default settings, seed 1 and a budget of N steps, when the run is started, then it stops after exactly N environment steps.
requirementTRAIN-02The same world, settings and seed on the same machine give an identical training run.
criteria
  • Given two runs with identical inputs, when both finish, then their logged results and final checkpoints are identical.
requirementTRAIN-03A run saves a checkpoint at 10 evenly spaced points in its budget, plus at the start (untrained) and the end.
criteria
  • Given a finished run, when its checkpoints are listed, then there are 12, at 0%, 10%, … 100% of the budget.
requirementTRAIN-04A run evaluates its current agent at every 5% of its budget on 20 episodes whose seeds are never used in training.
criteria
  • Given a finished run, when its evaluation log is read, then there are 21 evaluation points, each the mean score of 20 held-out-seed episodes.
requirementTRAIN-05Several runs of the same world with different seeds can be started together, one per processor core.
criteria
  • Given 5 seeds, when a batch of runs is started, then 5 runs proceed at the same time and each finishes with its own log and checkpoints.
requirementTRAIN-06Every learning method, before it is used in a world, learns the known best behavior in a tiny check world whose best behavior is worked out by hand.
criteria
  • Given a learning method and its check world, when it is trained for the check budget, then the trained agent picks the hand-derived best action in at least 95% of check-world states.
requirementTRAIN-07In worlds with more than one team, each team has its own learning method and settings, and all teams learn during the same run.
criteria
  • Given predator-prey with a learning method for each team, when a run proceeds, then both teams' agents change between consecutive checkpoints.

featureFISHFish tank
stagesS2
stage_viaStage line
source—
servesG1, G2, G3, G4
requirementFISH-01The tank is a square with walls; the ball moves in straight lines at constant speed and bounces off walls.
criteria
  • Given the ball heading into the right wall, when it touches the wall, then its sideways direction reverses and its speed is unchanged.
requirementFISH-02Each step a fish chooses a push as two numbers between −1 and 1 (left-right and up-down); its speed changes smoothly with the push, up to a top speed below the ball's.
criteria
  • Given a still fish pushing (1, 0) for several steps, when its speed is read, then it rises step by step toward its top speed, never above it.
requirementFISH-03When the ball touches a fish, the hit is counted and the fish reappears at a random spot at least one quarter of the tank's width from the ball.
criteria
  • Given the ball touching a fish, when the step ends, then the hit count is up by 1 and the fish is at least a quarter-width from the ball.
requirementFISH-04An episode lasts 1,000 steps; the score is hits per fish per episode (lower is better).
criteria
  • Given 4 fish and 6 total hits in an episode, when it ends, then the score is 1.5.
requirementFISH-05Variant "one fish": a single fish observes its own position and velocity and the ball's position and velocity.
criteria
  • Given the one-fish variant, when an observation is taken, then it holds those values for the fish and the ball.
requirementFISH-06Variant "many fish": 8 fish share the tank; each observes itself, the ball and the 3 nearest other fish.
criteria
  • Given the many-fish variant, when an observation is taken for any fish, then it holds its own state, the ball's and the 3 nearest fish's.
requirementFISH-07Variant "local sight": as "many fish", but a fish sees the ball and other fish only within a radius of one quarter of the tank's width; anything outside is reported as "not seen".
criteria
  • Given the ball beyond the radius, when a fish's observation is taken, then the ball's entries read "not seen".
requirementFISH-08Fish pass through each other; only the ball can hit them.
criteria
  • Given two fish moving onto the same spot, when the step ends, then both are there and no hit is counted.
requirementFISH-09The rule-based baseline pushes each fish directly away from the ball at full strength (from the ball it sees, or randomly when it sees none).
criteria
  • Given the ball to the fish's left, when the rule acts, then the push is (1, 0).
requirementFISH-10Every variant's trained fish beat both baselines as in EVAL-04 and EVAL-05.
criteria
  • Given 5 finished runs of each variant, when evaluated, then both baselines are beaten in each variant.
requirementFISH-11Fish tank runs through the same training runs, logging, replay viewer and evaluation as snake, and snake's results reproduce unchanged after fish tank is added.
criteria
  • Given a snake run repeated with its original settings and seed after fish tank exists, when it finishes, then its results match the original run exactly.

featurePREYPredator-prey
stagesS3
stage_viaStage line
source—
servesG1, G2, G3, G4
requirementPREY-01The arena is a square with walls, 6 prey, 2 predators and 10 food pellets; an eaten pellet reappears at a random spot after 50 steps.
criteria
  • Given seed 5, when an episode starts, then the counts are 6, 2 and 10, with no two objects touching.
requirementPREY-02Prey and predators move by smooth pushes as in FISH-02; predators' top speed is 1.2 × the prey's.
criteria
  • Given both at full speed, when their speeds are read, then the predator's is 1.2 × the prey's.
requirementPREY-03A prey touching a pellet eats it; a predator touching a prey catches it and the prey leaves the episode.
criteria
  • Given a predator touching a prey, when the step ends, then the prey is gone and the predator's catch count is up by 1.
requirementPREY-04An episode lasts 1,000 steps or until every prey is caught.
criteria
  • Given all 6 prey caught at step 600, when the step ends, then the episode is over.
requirementPREY-05Each team has its own score: prey score = pellets eaten per prey (higher is better); predator score = prey caught per episode (higher is better).
criteria
  • Given 18 pellets eaten and 3 prey caught, when the episode ends, then the prey score is 3 and the predator score is 3.
requirementPREY-06Every agent observes itself, and the nearest 2 food pellets, nearest 2 prey and nearest 2 predators within a radius of one third of the arena's width; anything outside reads "not seen".
criteria
  • Given a predator beyond the radius, when a prey's observation is taken, then that predator's entries read "not seen".
requirementPREY-07Rule-based baselines: prey flee the nearest seen predator, else move toward the nearest seen pellet; predators chase the nearest seen prey, else wander randomly.
criteria
  • Given a prey that sees a predator to its right, when the rule acts, then it pushes left.
requirementPREY-08Each trained team is evaluated against fixed opponents: trained prey against the rule-based predators, compared with random and rule-based prey; trained predators against the rule-based prey, compared with random and rule-based predators. Each passes as in EVAL-04 and EVAL-05.
criteria
  • Given 5 finished runs, when evaluated, then a separate verdict is shown for the prey team and the predator team.
requirementPREY-09The learner can play any prey checkpoint against any predator checkpoint from the same run, and see a grid of mean scores for every pairing of the 12 checkpoints of each side.
criteria
  • Given a finished run, when the pairing grid is made, then it shows a 12 × 12 grid of prey scores and of predator scores, each cell the mean of 20 held-out episodes.
requirementPREY-10The learning curve shows both teams' scores on the same step axis, so the back-and-forth between them is visible.
criteria
  • Given 5 finished runs, when the chart is made, then both teams' mean lines and bands appear against the same environment-steps axis.
requirementPREY-11Predator-prey runs through the same training runs, logging, replay viewer and evaluation as snake and fish tank, and their results reproduce unchanged after predator-prey is added.
criteria
  • Given a snake run and a fish run repeated with their original settings and seeds, when they finish, then their results match the originals exactly.

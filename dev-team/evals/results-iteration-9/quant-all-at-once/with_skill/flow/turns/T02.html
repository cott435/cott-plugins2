# Quant Research Platform

**Status:** Draft · **Last updated:** 2026-10-17

## The problem
I want to learn quant research on US equities properly and find out whether any signal survives honest testing. Ad-hoc research fools itself easily. Stocks that later delisted go missing, models peek at the future, and "out of sample" means something different in every experiment. Most results that look good are chance. Without a trustworthy dataset and a disciplined way to test claims, I can't tell a real finding from a lucky one.

## Who it's for
Me: one person doing personal research and simulated paper trading, with no real money.

## Goals
1. Every result rests on daily US equity data I can trust: index membership as it was on each date, delisted stocks included, corporate actions applied, and every download validated.
2. "Out of sample" means one thing everywhere: every method is scored on the same walk-forward test periods, with a gap so training never sees test outcomes.
3. No claim without a test: each hypothesis is written down before it is run, tested against chance, and its outcome recorded, failures included.
4. A new method earns its place only by beating the simple benchmark out of sample. Classical risk-based portfolios are first judged on realised variance. Anything driven by machine learning must then beat them.
5. I can explore the dataset, and watch downloads and validation runs progress live, in a local browser app.

## Constraints
- One person on a laptop, with an occasionally rented GPU for neural networks.
- An existing Sharadar price subscription; any other data must be free or under $50 a month.
- Daily data only, for the S&P 500 with historical membership on each date.
- All analysis in Python. The browser app is TypeScript and React, with typed contracts to the Python side.
- Simulated backtests only. No brokerage connection.

## What success looks like
- The dataset reproduces S&P 500 membership history, with delisted members present and checked.
- Covariance estimators and classical portfolios are ranked on realised out-of-sample variance, and the ranking is backed by a test.
- A ledger in which every feature and model claim has its stated test and outcome.
- A clear answer to whether any signal beats the classical portfolios after costs. "None does" counts as a successful answer.

## What we're betting on
- The existing subscription, plus at most $50 a month, covers delisted stocks and membership history. If not, Goal 1 needs rethinking.
- Around 500 large-cap stocks over the available history give enough data for tests with real power.
- A laptop and occasional GPU rental are enough compute.

## Not doing
- Real money or a broker connection: research and simulated trading only.
- Intraday data: daily is enough for these questions.
- A universe beyond S&P 500 history, for now: cleaner, affordable data first.
- A shared or hosted app: it runs locally, for me only.
- Return prediction before risk estimation is right: variance comes first.
- Open-ended trading agents: they stop if they can't beat minimum variance after costs.

## Open questions
- How many years of delisted-stock and membership history does the data actually cover? Short history weakens every test.
